  2. Set the trip’s course. Is the goal relaxation, sightseeing, activity, food, etc.?
  3. Come up with options Don’t let yourself depend on just one location.
  4. Check transport and accommodation together Room is only good if transport to get to room is not a nightmare.
  5. Check your plans and the conditions Look at the weather, Look at travel information, Look at the activities.
