Expedia Hotels for Finding the Right Stay in New Zealand

Expedia Hotels can help travelers get a general idea of prices for New Zealand hotels, but treating the lowest price as if it's the most value is a habit they should break, as it can get very expensive in a country where hotels can help travelers avoid wasting time driving, and where weather can affect travel plans. Additionally, travelers shouldn't overlook the value of booking a hotel with amenities that can help them avoid day trips, especially in New Zealand where room type and cancellation policies can make a big difference in travel itineraries. Expedia Hotels should be used more as a comparison tool rather than an automatic booking decision for travelers going to New Zealand. Travelers should be looking to put hotels, serviced apartments, lodges, and more against their booked travel plans. For example, in New Zealand, parking is a premium, and a property somewhere close to an amenity that offers parking could be more valuable than a cheap hotel. So then, travelers going to New Zealand should see value in booking hotels that make their travel plans easier, not the ones that are the cheapest. Booking a hotel with parking close to an amenity should be simpler with the use of Expedia Hotels, as long as travelers avoid booking the cheapest hotel without looking at amenities and cancellation policies.

In New Zealand, Location Really Is the Most Valuable Amenity

Advice for booking hotels often say to look at the map after you look at the prices, but for New Zealand trips this advice is incorrect. Location should be one of the filters because good base location generally affects transport costs, daily timing, dining, how flexible you can be, and how you can best meet unexpected needs.

In more metropolitan areas, an out of core cheaper listing can look attractive. When you start to consider all the ancillary costs of traveling in and out, that may not be the case.

In Auckland or Wellington, saving money on accommodation to travel further may not be the better option. The time and monetary value of convenience can mean more. Walking to dinner and other recreational activities may mean you can be more flexible without having to consider every single trip and its logistics.

For a traveler on a road trip who would value the peacefulness of the location and has vehicle access, this trade off may work. For travelers hoping for more convenience and accessibility, it may be a deterrent.

From an Expedia Hotels standpoint, it is advised to review the map and consider location prior to being attracted to a rate.

Before confirming through Expedia Hotels, review the cancellation time period and any conditions for booking changes. Review the timing of payment as well as the room and rate and ensure that you understand what is being booked. “Free cancellation” does not equate to cancellation on the day of booking. Policies are different for bookings for different rooms at the same hotel. The terms associated with the booking option you choose take precedence over the overall score for a property and its associated pictures. We aren't looking to find a hotel without criticism. We are looking to see if the complaints matter. Take a hotel booking made for a quiet break. Those complaints of noise should be taken seriously, regardless of the overall good feedback for the hotel. For those traveling with a car, those comments on the practicality of parking should also be of concern. For those travelers concerned with the connectivity of their itinerary, it would not suffice to see that a hotel is marked as “business friendly”. They would also need to look at the reviews. At the same time, travelers should not be looking at the reviews as a checklist to find critical flaws. One off situations should also not be given more weight than clear evidence on a hotel. Expedia Hotels are the most constructive reviews when users utilize their reviews as a means to confirm or disprove a shortlist formed based on other criteria. Match the accommodation type to the trip - and not to a vacation fantasy. New Zealand is able to host an abundance of traveling styles. These may include short city trips, multiple stop trips, family trips, business trips, multi-stop trips, and more. Expedia Hotels are wonderful resources in that they merge all styles of accommodation and put them at your finger tips. Travelers should not make the assumption that a hotel is always the answer. A good case in point is that when making a trip for a short city break, a hotel in the city center can be ideal. For travelers with gear, an apartment hotel or a hotel with the added convenience of a kitchen can be ideal. Options include lodges or other stays with more privacy for itineraries designed around views and seclusion. There's no best option. Each one provides a different trade off in terms of daily convenience. The most convenient is the format that eliminates the most trade off.

This is where travelers tend to get into problems. People often book an out of the way property that looks amazing in the photos, but then every meal, activity, and transfer requires lots of planning. Others book a compact room that looks amazing, and then realize the room is a cramped living space that makes it hard to want to stay longer. Expedia Hotels offer great ways to book, but travelers have to be a bit more realistic with their booking based on how they can actually travel rather than how amazing the hotel looks in the photos.

Travelers have to think realistically. Are you using the room for rest periods during your busy travel days, or are there downtime activities at the property you actually want to do? Do you have to rent a car? Are there kids and several travelers? Are you carrying lots of luggage? Are you doing work on the road, or do you need to plan for early mornings? Is your room going to be shared with several people? The answers to these questions are more likely to help you find a room than a bunch of labels.

Use Comparison Tools, and Then Double Check

Expedia Hotels can help narrow down lots of search options, especially if travelers have lots of options when it comes to destinations and dates in New Zealand. Adjusting search preferences for room choices, comparing amenities, reviews and policies, then drilling down to the details can help travelers narrow down their selections to some solid options. The comparison can help, but only if it is followed by verification.

Before booking, make sure you look at the dates, the number of guests you are booking for, room category, numbers and configuration of beds, features, price, and policy. Just because you will need to arrive earlier, because you have specific accessibility needs, or because you absolutely need a facility, doesn’t mean you shouldn’t take the time to review the descriptions for each accommodation to see if they meet your needs. While booking platforms tend to simplify and consolidate information, you are still responsible for ensuring the specific elements needed for your trip are met. Similar things should be compared to other similar things. Do not say that a non-refundable room without a parking space across the city is better than a room in the center with breakfast. This comparison isitudes how the room actually adds value to the stay. Just look at similar room policies and then decide if a higher price worth it. You should also avoid feeling a need to make all bookings at once. For a trip with multiple stops, it is a good idea to book the stays for the important, fixed part of the trip, while the other bookings remain flexible, based on the conditions that are available. This is better than the extreme of booking everything at least a few months in advance or not booking anything until the last minute. ### Better Expedia Hotels Booking Method for New Zealand To simplify what has worked for us for New Zealand, think of the purpose of the stop: is it an overnight stay, a break on the way, a base from which to do an activity, something long and more restful, or the last stop before you leave? Once you have the purpose, determine the location that serves this purpose easiest. Then, use the practical filters for what you need: accessibility, flexible cancellations, parking, kitchen, family space, etc. Fourth, look into total booking conditions and room details rather than just the nightly rate. Finally, take the time to read recent reviews to ensure there are no issues that may impact your stay as well. This method will likely take more time then simply sorting by price and booking the best available option; but, it will provide much better outcomes. Accommodations should not be viewed as an isolated cost. Accommodations set the tone for the remainder of your entire trip. A suboptimal choice for accommodations can result in a frustrating daily hassle, whereas an optimal choice can make even the most demanding travel plans easy. Expedia Hotels’ primary value is not that it takes away the need for judgment. It provides travelers with a place to make better comparisons. In New Zealand, the best choice is rarely the room with the lowest number adjacent to it. Rather it is the stay that suits the planned itinerary, fits the traveler’s needs, and is flexible enough to accommodate the needs of the traveler’s trip.
