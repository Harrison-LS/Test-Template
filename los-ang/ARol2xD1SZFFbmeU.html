Content

Fillmore District is one of San Francisco's most attractive and entertaining areas, where you can experience local culture, enjoy fine food, and choose from a wealth of entertainment activities. If you're looking to buy travel souvenirs, Fillmore has many shops worth visiting! Today we've carefully selected four great shopping destinations in Fillmore – come along!


French Dessert Shop B. Patisserie


Address: 2821 California St.


B. Patisserie is a unique French-style dessert shop in Fillmore – after shopping here, stopping by for coffee and pastries can definitely erase your day's fatigue! The shop's homemade gingerbread, ham and cheese croissants are signature items, plus their seasonal limited-time coffee cakes all taste wonderful. On weekends, you might try the social media-famous macarons or apple pies handcrafted over ten hours.


African Mosaic Gallery


Address: 1305 Fillmore St.


This art gallery houses many extraordinary African treasures, founded by Ivorian artist Solange Mallett. The exhibits include textiles, beads, decorative arts, jewelry and other artworks created by artists from across the African continent.

You can also admire African tribal masks, sculptures, and necklaces here, as well as handcrafted artworks such as African clothing, specialty bags, and beads.


Browser Bookstore


Address: 2195 Fillmore St.


"Browser" means to search, and as its name suggests, Browser Bookstore is perfect for tourists who want to browse bookstores looking for good books. Since 1976 until now, this place has attracted visitors from both local and around the world. There are various subject matters of books here, but the real treasure here is its philosophy and religion collection. Browser has many Buddhist-related books, as well as many books about Eastern and Western religions. If you're interested in this area, Browser is worth exploring.


Roam Artisan Burgers


Roam Artisan Burgers


Address: 1923 Fillmore St.


After a day of shopping, the most desired thing is to find a local delicacy to enjoy heartily. In Fillmore, Roam Artisan Burgers is absolutely a great place.

Roam Artisan Burger is located on Fillmore Street, known as the best burger spot in Fillmore. This classic-style burger joint offers various flavors including beef, turkey, bison, vegetable, or lamb, along with all kinds of toppings. Additionally, their handcrafted sodas and milkshakes make for perfect burger companions - absolutely worth trying for great taste.
