Drawing from traditions similar to Catholic churches, an important component of St. Mary's Cathedral design that reflects the experience such churches evoke is the stained glass windows distributed throughout all sides of the building.

This St. Mary's Cathedral was designed by Pietro Belluschi and Pier Luigi Nervi.

Fisherman's Wharf area along the coast produces delicious seafood including crabs, shrimp, abalone, squid, sea urchins, salmon, mackerel, and cod. The best time to enjoy fresh seafood is between November of one year and June of the next year.


Although there's no fine soft sand beach, the multitude of shops, uniquely flavored restaurants, and exciting street performances here are enough to give you a leisurely yet fulfilling afternoon.

San Francisco was originally a Spanish colony, became Mexican territory after Mexico gained independence, and then returned to America after the Mexican-American War.
