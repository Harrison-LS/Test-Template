IV. What types of tickets exist? How should you choose?


Disney offers several ticket categories: 1-day passes, 2-day passes, multi-day tickets, and annual memberships. Children under 3 enter free, ages 3-9 require child tickets, and anyone 10 or older needs adult tickets.


You can purchase directly through California Disney's official website, though this requires payment with an international credit card. The site will prompt account registration via email for booking purposes. After successful purchase, you'll receive your electronic ticket within 24 hours via email—monitor closely to confirm delivery. If there's a delay in ticket generation, contact support promptly through phone or email. Alternatively, consider purchasing from travel agencies at potentially lower prices than the official website.


Upon receiving your electronic ticket like the example shown below, carefully verify its type and validity period. Keep a digital copy on your phone—entry simply requires scanning the QR code!


Sample of Disney electronic ticket


Tips:

Disney tickets are divided into Value (off-season), Regular (weekday/regular season), and Peak (high season) categories, with a one-year validity period. Please note that Value season tickets cannot be used during Regular or Peak seasons; Regular season tickets cannot be used during Peak season. If you use tickets across different seasons, you need to pay the price difference at the park entrance to enter; prices are as charged by the attraction area.


1) 1-Day Tickets


If you only plan to stay at Disney for one day, there are two ticket options available for you:

1. [1-Day Single Park Ticket]

This ticket allows you to choose and visit one park between Disneyland and California Adventure. The different colors in the image below represent Value (off-season), Regular, and Peak season tickets respectively—each with distinct pricing that changes daily.


2. [1-Day Double Park Ticket]

This ticket allows you to freely visit both parks within one day, costing slightly more than single-park tickets by a few hundred dollars. While it permits access to both parks, it can be challenging to experience everything in such a short timeframe. Therefore, fewer people purchase this type of ticket. However, if you have abundant energy or are an experienced visitor who only wants to experience select attractions, this option might be worth considering.

2) 2-Day Tickets


If you plan to spend two days at Disney? There are similarly two ticket options available.

1. [2-Day Single Park Ticket]

This is the top recommended and one of the best-selling ticket types! Purchasing this ticket means you can visit a different park each day. Not only can you experience all the must-do attractions, but you'll also have plenty of time to enjoy them at a relaxed pace. Most visitors who choose this ticket spend one day at Disney California Adventure Park and one day at Disneyland Resort. Of course, if you have a special attachment to one particular park, feel free to stay there for both days!


2、【2-Day 2-Park Ticket】

Selecting this two-day, two-park ticket means you have more flexible options. At any time, you can freely enter and exit both parks. 1. Monte Cristo Sandwich

Don't think this is just an ordinary fried ham and cheese sandwich. Though it looks unremarkable, it's actually a popular dish at Cafe Orleans, a New Orleans specialty restaurant. After frying the sandwich with meat and cheese inside, it's dusted with a thin layer of powdered sugar and served with blueberry sauce and fresh fruit. The texture is crispy on the outside and soft on the inside without being greasy, with sweetness enhancing the savory flavor of the fried sandwich—it's definitely worth your money. Plus, Cafe Orleans' fries are extraordinary: topped with freshly grated Parmesan cheese, minced garlic, and basil, they're golden and fragrant. Combined with New Orleans' Creole sauce, they're simply irresistible. This is reportedly a popular Disney snack item! The average price ranges from $15 to $35.

11. What are some good hotel recommendations?


If you want to stay inside Disney, the hotels provide convenient quick-access entrance channels and early park entry privileges. The prices are slightly higher than outside hotels. But that unique feeling of staying within fairy tale world is truly irreplaceable!


Recommended Disney On-Site Hotels:

1、California Hotel & Spa Disney's Grand Californian Hotel & Spa

This charming hotel offers premium accommodation and impeccable service, with equally elegant architectural design. It features a luxurious spa, along with award-winning dining and wine experiences. After having a wild day at Disneyland, you can relax your body and soul with a comfortable night's stay here.


2、Disneyland Hotel

This is a hotel themed around roller skating, located just steps away from both Disneyland Park and California Adventure. The design of rooms and restaurants is filled with Disney's whimsical colors. There is even a park-themed water play area within the hotel where you can experience the thrill of roller skating!


3、Disney's Paradise Pier Hotel

If you want to experience a seaside vacation feel, choose this hotel. It features a rooftop pool design - not only can you swim freely in the rooftop pool, but you can also share breakfast with cute Disney characters, and even watch Disney's fireworks performance from within the hotel. A pretty nice experience!


Actually, there are some quite good hotels around Disneyland that aren't far away and offer reasonable prices. Here I recommend several Disneyland off-site hotels with great overall value.


Recommended Hotels Near Disney:

1. Best Western Plus Hotel

This hotel offers an excellent location, just a 5-minute walk from the main entrance of Disney Park. It provides free hot buffet breakfast, complimentary guest parking, and a fitness center. Additionally, you can directly watch the fireworks show at Disney from the hotel's public terrace! Great value for money; double beds are around 1148 yuan.


2. Grand Legacy At The Park Hotel

The hotel is only 400 meters from Disney California Adventure Park and features an outdoor heated swimming pool. Coffee makers are provided in guest rooms so you can make your own coffee. For the convenience of younger children, some rooms offer cribs and fold-out beds. Additionally, the rooftop lounge is a signature feature of the hotel, filled with warmth and romance! The lounge serves cocktails, craft beer, and snack platters. You can enjoy Disney's fireworks show right here from this venue. Prices are very reasonable; double beds are around 1012 yuan.


3. Hotel Fullerton Anaheim

If you don't specifically need to stay right at the Disney entrance, this hotel is also quite nice. It's not far from Disneyland at all, only about a 15-minute drive, and offers free shuttle service to Disneyland. Guests can purchase Disneyland tickets directly at the hotel. The hotel provides hot buffet breakfast daily, and there are many delicious options available at the on-site bar and grill restaurant. Guests who have stayed here all feel it has great value for money, plus the location is excellent with convenient transportation! A double room costs around 1182 yuan.


XII. Helpful Tips and Things to Note


1. Pack lightly. Wear water-resistant, comfortable shoes. Girls, absolutely no skirts!

2. Protect against sun exposure and rain. Do your sunscreen work well in advance; buying disposable raincoats is also very necessary.

3. When enjoying thrilling rides, secure your personal items and glasses properly, follow all safety procedures, and girls should tie their hair back.

4. The shuttle tram between the parking lot and park gates will continue operating for about one hour after the park closes.

5. Do NOT take photos or record video while watching any Disney theater performances!

6. Lockers are provided in the park where you can store necessary items safely and conveniently.

7. Smoking is permitted only in designated areas within the park.
