6 spectacular super blooms worth the trip

Until I saw a superbloom, I thought I knew what a desert looked like. With the right conditions, a desert can turn from bone dry and full of rocks to a sea of flowers. Superblooms happen when rain finally falls in deserts after long periods of time and dormant seeds for flowers for years suddenly spring to life and start growing. There are a lot of places that have stunning flower blooms and are known for them. One of the most well known places is California’s Death Valley but there are a lot more places to see them all over the world and they are so much more worth it to go see in person.
