Climb to Cliffs Lighthouse and Spectacular Pigeon Island Rocks


After crossing the Golden Gate Bridge and turning left, you arrive at Point Bonita Lighthouse (7.5/10)

A few steps after parking will lead you to this large rock formation in Figure Two

Walking through a small path inside the rock formation gives an open feeling. At this point you can see the lighthouse in Figure Three~


This is the third and final lighthouse established within San Francisco Bay, coming after Alcatraz and Fort Point

In 1853 a Carrier Pigeon ship ran aground here, so it was renamed Pigeon Point. To prevent accidents, this lighthouse was built in 1872

But pay attention! This beautiful lighthouse is only open to the public from Saturday to Monday afternoons from 12:30 PM to 3:30 PM

The lighthouse sits on an independent rock formation, accessible only by a small bridge. Be careful!

Pictures really cannot capture how it looks in person. Surrounding the bridge are waves and cliffs. Purple flowers bloom on the left side while the tiny Golden Gate Bridge appears in the distance

In any case, it's just very beautiful (○'ω'○)

Cliff beach, mountain covered in wildflowers, dog paradise~


Fort Funston (9.5/10) Located at the southwest corner of San Francisco, next to San Francisco State University

Here you can find a peninsula extending into the Pacific Ocean, breezes sweeping through, steep rocky trails, dogs, and coastal beaches - perfect for hiking, paragliding, and horseback riding


After exiting the parking lot, you arrive at a cliffside covered with wildflowers (photos two and three)

Looking down from above, the scenery is very beautiful. Beach, sunshine - once you're here you absolutely don't want to leave

Follow the only path and walk and walk, you'll see so many dogs~ One person walks a dozen or more at once

The dogs are all gentle with leashes. Big dogs wear masks~ So no need to be scared. There's even a drinking spot for the dogs, very cute. Missing my dog already ●︿●

Walk down this slope in photo five and you'll reach the beach~ On that day the wildflowers were all blooming, super beautiful~ In the distance you can also see San Francisco from afar

Feels like not many people know about this beach. It's relatively clean with fewer people. Walk and walk and you'll see a long string of dog paw prints~

To walk across such a long stretch of beach, you need to climb up a small hill. I haven't exercised in a while and climbed until I was exhausted (￣(●●)￣)


Still cliff, wildflower beach~


Mussel Rock Park (7.5/10) Just a little further from that cliff beach~

Cliff forest trail, seaside labyrinth, distant view of Golden Gate Bridge


In the upper left corner of San Francisco there is a park called Lands End - its greenery looks particularly like a forest~

Most people go to Ocean Beach. By chance I walked up a few steps and felt like discovering a treasure: Sutro Baths (9/10)


Passing Cliff House restaurant (there's a huge floor-to-ceiling window in the dining area; seating by the window allows you to overlook the Pacific Ocean, extremely beautiful - though I've heard it's slightly expensive. Hope to try it sometime~) leads you to Sutro Baths


Sutro Baths was a private ocean swimming venue built on the coast. Due to huge operational and maintenance costs being forced to close.

A fire in 1966 burned this place down. Now only the remaining concrete framework and pipes allow one to imagine its former glory

Walking to the far right you can even see a heart-shaped rock~


To the right of Sutro Baths there's a staircase leading up - first is a lush little grove, then it becomes a trail that's half cliff and half forest

Reaching Lands End Labyrinth (9/10) opens up your view. Have to say this seaside labyrinth still has quite an impact

Also a 40-50 meter high cliff. Standing at the top looking down on the labyrinth, to the right is the magnificent Golden Gate Bridge, to the left are cliffs and beach

Colorful mosaic stairs - a photographer's paradise


Below Golden Gate Park lies an incredibly beautiful staircase called 16th Avenue Tiled Steps (8.5/10), built through donations from nearby residents

Fragments of stained glass were shattered then rearranged into patterns; this staircase depicts the Milky Way


At the top is the Moon River and Sun - reportedly glowing softly at night~

Recently redesigned with a succulent theme, featuring an abundance of succulents

Perfect for photography, though it gets crowded during peak hours

Note~ After climbing up, walk a few more steps to ascend another staircase that reaches the top of Grandview Park

From here you can overlook Golden Gate Park and the Pacific Ocean


There's also a smaller mosaic staircase~ called Hidden Garden Steps (8/10), located right beside the one above

This theme features plants with many mushrooms and little animals in vibrant colors, great for photos

However, the stairs are shorter, so there aren't small groves to view from afar after climbing up~

De Young Museum gift shop and free viewing deck


The De Young (7/10) Art Museum in Golden Gate Park should be well known to everyone~

I'm not sure if anyone noticed the museum also has a high-level free viewing deck plus gift shop

The viewing deck is on the far right when you enter, no ticket needed to get in~ Just follow the crowd and take the elevator up to around 8-9 floors


The viewing deck offers 360-degree views with good quality. The glass is quite large and clean, so the scenery is excellent~ You can overlook most of San Francisco from above

In the middle is a small gift shop selling pretty exquisite items but they're a bit pricey = =

The important thing is it's free! Also nearby are the California Academy of Sciences and Japanese Tea Garden which you can visit together


Fun and interesting Walt Disney Museum

The magnificent Grace Cathedral that passes by on the streetcar


Board the cable car at the last stop on California Street in downtown

Go up a big hill, pass through Chinatown, and you can arrive at this magnificent Grace Cathedral~

I've never been to Europe, so this is one of the better cathedrals I've visited

The main entrance is a protected historical artifact with various reliefs on it. I heard they're gilded

Highly recommended to visit during the day~ When there's light, the stained glass windows look particularly beautiful

There are prayer services on weekends. The people praying seemed so sincere and devout that it was a bit hard for me as an atheist to fully understand

Still, it's a worthwhile place for sightseeing

Modern architecture art museum


San Francisco Contemporary Jewish Museum is pretty close to sfmoma.

The building has a modern feel - a 3D cube slanted into the ground with many skylights.

Each museum has free days for the public or boa cardholders, you can check dates before going.

This museum's exhibitions change every three months. When I went it was "Generations: Inherited Memories and Contemporary Art."

The museum works hard to connect art, humanities, memories, and history - making people more sensitive and attentive to "historical memories"

It's actually quite small inside but the exhibits are still pretty good.


Great nighttime view spot in SoMa - Alcatraz at night


This place doesn't need much introduction haha~ It's basically a must-visit attraction in San Francisco.

Tickets need to be booked in advance, it gets quite popular.

You can book on the official website or buy tickets on-site. Daytime seems to be $40 per person and night is $55 (I already forgot 0 0).

You take a boat there and can also see the BART along the way~


The island has become a historic district - you can't pick flowers or pick up stones as souvenirs.

Near the entrance you can individually pick up a guide device, it even has Chinese!

You can see rows of small rooms in the old prison (really tiny - small bed + toilet + sink, one cage per person).

Can also see the hole dug by four escaped prisoners, the inmates' cafeteria, and isolation cells.

Following the automatic guide device makes it easy to tour completely. The narration is really good.

Different from daytime, at night you can see San Francisco's night view~ Still very beautiful.

Remember to bring a coat! It gets quite cold with strong wind at night~
