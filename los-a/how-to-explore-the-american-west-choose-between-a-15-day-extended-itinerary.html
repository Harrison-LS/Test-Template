San Francisco

How to explore the American West? Choose between a 15-day extended itinerary or a 10-day shorter route!

Many people are certain that the western United States should be their first destination when visiting the country. However, with its wide variety of attractions scattered across a vast area, planning the itinerary and allocating time can be the most challenging part. Don't worry—this guide will introduce you to the must-see attractions of the American West and teach you how to enjoy a loop tour without retracing your route!


A Map to Understand Travel Destinations in the American West


The western United States includes 11 states, centered around four major cities—Los Angeles, San Francisco, Las Vegas, and Seattle—and complemented by numerous spectacular national parks. The region has a mild and pleasant climate. It is not only the heart of the latest global entertainment trends but also home to world-famous national parks such as Yellowstone, the Grand Canyon, and Yosemite. The American West Coast is a microcosm and reflection of American culture. As you explore the West Coast, a sense of ease, freedom, and relaxation will likely be your greatest takeaway. Please note that public transportation in the western United States is underdeveloped, so driving is recommended.


Introduction to the Must-Visit Cities and Attractions of the American West


Los Angeles - The City of Angels


Introduction: Los Angeles is located in Southern California on the West Coast of the United States. It is the country's second-largest city after New York, a must-visit destination in the American West, a cultural and artistic capital, a gathering place for influencers, and a paradise for theme parks. Surrounded by mountains on three sides and facing the ocean on the fourth, the city lies in a broad basin.

Recommended travel period: Year-round. Los Angeles does not have distinctly defined seasons, and there are nearly 300 sunny days each year. If you prefer to avoid excessive heat, it is recommended to avoid July and August, although this period is ideal for water activities.

Attractions


Representative attraction:

1. Universal Studios Hollywood

Introduction: Universal Studios Hollywood is an original movie-themed amusement park located on the grounds of a film and television studio in the northwestern suburbs of downtown Los Angeles. It is a must-visit destination for travelers to Los Angeles. In addition to movie-related rides and attractions and The Wizarding World of Harry Potter, the park also offers a Studio Tour that takes you behind the scenes of Universal Studios' production facilities and to several real filming locations.

Address: 100 Universal City Plaza, Universal City, CA 91608, USA

Ticket prices: General admission $109-$129; Express tickets with skip-the-line access $179-$259

Opening hours: 10:00-18:00. The park's daily operating hours vary and may open later or close earlier due to special events. Please refer to the official park website for the latest schedule.

Transportation: Take the Red Line subway and get off at Universal City Station. A free shuttle is available a short distance from the station exit and takes 2-3 minutes to reach Universal Studios.

2. Disneyland Park, California

Introduction: Located in Anaheim, Disneyland Park is the world's first Disney theme park and the only one built under the supervision of Disney founder Walt Disney. It is filled with traditional Disney charm. At this theme park, you will experience more dreamlike and entertaining adventures than thrilling and exhilarating ones.

Address: 1313 Disneyland Dr, Anaheim, CA 92802, USA

Ticket prices: One-day adult ticket $104; child ticket $98. Two-day adult ticket $225; child ticket $210. One-day Park Hopper ticket $154.

Opening hours: 08:00–00:00 the following day (Disneyland Park), 08:00–22:00 (Disney California Adventure Park). Opening hours may vary by season; please refer to the official website for the latest information.

Transportation: Take buses 43, 83, 430, or 460 and get off at the Disneyland stop.

3. Santa Monica

Introduction: Santa Monica is the beach closest to downtown Los Angeles and the western terminus of Route 66. Its ocean views are beloved by Hollywood directors. It is also home to Pacific Park, the only amusement park on the West Coast located over the ocean. Countless tourists visit every year and cannot resist spending time enjoying the rides at Pacific Park, making Santa Monica the top choice for visitors to Los Angeles seeking to visit the beach.

Address: Pacific Coast Hwy, Santa Monica, CA 90401, USA

Ticket prices: Free

Opening hours: Open all day

Transportation: Take buses 20, 33, or 534 and get off at the Ocean / Colorado stop.

Official attraction website: ***


4. Getty Center

Introduction: The Getty Center is an important iconic cultural landmark in Los Angeles and one of the museums with the richest collections in California and even the world, making it a destination admired by art lovers. It houses a large collection of European paintings, sculptures, decorative arts, and photographs. Moreover, the architecture of the Getty Center itself is a work of art. Visitors need to take a tram up the mountain, while enjoying panoramic views of the entire valley. Outside the museum is the colorful Getty Central Garden, whose reputation is no less impressive than the artworks inside the museum. Shaded paths, gently flowing streams, and brooks winding along the paths into ponds create a picturesque setting. At the center of the pond is an azalea maze, and the atmosphere throughout is infused with art.

Address: 1200 Getty Center Dr, Los Angeles, CA 90049

Admission: Free

Opening hours: Tuesday–Friday and Sunday, 10:00–17:30; Saturday, 10:00–21:00; closed to the public on Mondays

Transportation: You can drive there or take Bus 234 and get off at the Sepulveda / Getty Center stop. Visitors can only park in the parking lot at the foot of the mountain, then take the tram to and from the hilltop art center.

Official website: ***


5. OUE Skyspace Observation Deck

Introduction: OUE Skyspace is California's highest open-air observation deck, located inside the U.S. Bank Tower in downtown Los Angeles. Rising more than 1,000 feet above the ground, it offers a panoramic 360-degree view of the City of Angels. Here, you can sit quietly on a small indoor sofa by the window and enjoy the sunset, or step outside to get up close to the surrounding skyscrapers. The outdoor observation deck is enclosed by transparent floor-to-ceiling glass, providing an exceptionally unobstructed view. It also features the popular 70-story exterior glass slide and angel wings, perfect for check-in photos. OUE Skyspace is an emerging landmark that should not be missed when visiting Los Angeles.

Address: 633 W 5th St #840, Los Angeles, CA 90071

Ticket prices: Adult admission $25; child admission $19. Adult admission plus slide ticket $33; child admission plus slide ticket $27.

Opening hours: 10:00-22:00

Transportation: Take buses 16, 17, 18, 53, 55, 62, 316, or 355 and get off at 5th & Grand. It is a one-minute walk from there.

San Francisco - Vacation Paradise


Introduction: Mention the Golden Gate Bridge, cable cars, Alcatraz Island, and Fisherman's Wharf, and San Francisco immediately comes to mind. Yet the city is far more than these familiar highlights. San Francisco has more than 40 neighborhoods, each with its own distinctive character. It combines modern and classical beauty and has repeatedly been named one of the most beautiful cities in the United States. Relaxed and leisurely, it is a vacation paradise for people in the western United States.

Best time to travel: Year-round. Generally, December to February is the rainy season, while June and July have the most comfortable temperatures.

Attraction locations


1. Golden Gate Bridge

Introduction: The Golden Gate Bridge was built using more than 100,000 tons of steel. It is a landmark of San Francisco and the entire United States, as well as a miracle of modern bridge engineering. The orange-red bridge spans San Francisco Bay and the Golden Gate Strait, creating a magnificent and spectacular sight. It is also a popular filming location frequently featured in movies and television dramas.

Address: Golden Gate Bridge, San Francisco, CA, USA

Admission: Free

Opening hours: All day

Transportation: Take bus routes 2/4/8/10/18/24/27/38/44/54/56/58/70/72/72X/74/76/92/93/97/101/101X and get off at the Golden Gate Bridge Toll Plaza stop.

Official website: ***


2. Lombard Street

Introduction: Lombard Street is known as the most crooked street in the world. With eight hairpin turns along a short stretch and lush greenery and flowers lining the road, it has become an iconic San Francisco landmark. From the upper section of the street, visitors can also enjoy distant views of the Bay Bridge and Coit Tower, creating a breathtaking panorama.

Address: Lombard Street, San Francisco, California

Admission: Free

Opening hours: All day

Transportation: Take the Powell-Hyde Cable Car Line and get off at the Lombard St & Hyde St stop

Official website: ***


3. Fisherman's Wharf

Introduction: The name Fisherman's Wharf is widely known. It stretches from Ghirardelli Square to Pier 35, all of which is considered part of the wharf area. Among them, Pier 39 is the liveliest, with a variety of distinctive shops, restaurants, and street performances nearby. Visitors can also watch sea lions and enjoy distant views of Alcatraz Island, the Golden Gate Bridge, and the Bay Bridge.

Address: Fisherman's Wharf, Pier 39, San Francisco, CA 94133, America

Admission: Free

Opening hours: All day

Transportation: Take buses 2, 4, 8, 18, 24, 27, 38, 44, 54, 56, 58, 72, 74, or 76 and get off at the Beach St & Taylor St stop, then walk for 2 minutes. Alternatively, take the F Market light rail line and get off at the Beach St & Stockton St stop or the The Embarcadero & Stockton St stop.

Official website: ***


4. Alcatraz Island

Introduction: Alcatraz Island is a rocky island covering 22 acres. From the 1930s to the 1960s, it was used as a federal prison to incarcerate prisoners who had committed serious crimes. In the 1970s, Alcatraz ended its “career” as a prison, and in 1986 it was designated a National Historic Landmark of the United States. Today, the entire island has preserved as much of its original prison appearance as possible. Visitors can still see the remains of the prison cells, dining hall, lighthouse, water tower, and warden’s office, making it a haunting yet essential stop on any trip to San Francisco.

Address: Alcatraz Island, San Francisco, CA

Ticket prices: Day tour: adults $37.25, youth $37.25, seniors $35.25, children $23. Night tour: adults $37, youth $36, seniors $34.25, children $21.75.

Opening hours: 8:45–21:25

Transportation: Take a ferry from the San Francisco waterfront

Official website: ***


Highway 1 - Coastal Road Trip


Introduction: This is one of the best roads for experiencing the American West, featuring endless coastlines, red wine, delicious food, and historic attractions. After the challenges of 2017, California’s Highway 1 was once again fully reopened in 2018!

Recommended travel time: Year-round

Notable attractions:

1. 17-Mile Drive

Introduction: 17-Mile Drive is the most famous tourist attraction on Highway 1. It is surrounded by the renowned Pebble Beach Resort, with the Pacific Ocean immediately to the west and south and the Del Monte cypress forest to the east. Along the route, you can enjoy blue seas and skies, white sandy beaches, and a rugged, winding mountain road, with extraordinary pine trees and rocks creating breathtaking scenery at every turn.

Address: 17 Mile Dr, Pacific Grove, CA 93950

Admission: $9.25–$10 vehicle fee; free for pedestrians and cyclists

Opening hours: All day

How to get there: Drive yourself or join a tour

Official website: ***17-mile-drive


2. Solvang

Introduction: Solvang means “sunny countryside” in Danish, and this small town truly enjoys clear and pleasant weather throughout the year, attracting many visitors. It is a Danish-style town in the United States created by Danish immigrants in the early 20th century, who transplanted the architectural style and community planning of their homeland. When the King of Denmark visited the United States, he made a special visit here.

Address: 320 Alisal Mesa Road, Solvang, CA 93463, USA

Admission: Free

Opening hours: All day

Transportation: Drive 128 miles along US-101 N from downtown Los Angeles; the journey takes approximately two hours

Official website: ***


3. Bixby Creek Bridge

Introduction: If one landmark were to represent California State Route 1, it would have to be Bixby Creek Bridge. Completed in 1932, this bridge marked the completion of the entire California State Route 1. Bixby Bridge is an open-spandrel single-arch bridge, the longest concrete arch bridge in California's highway system, and one of the area's most popular attractions.

Address: Bixby Creek Bridge, Monterey, CA 93940, USA

Admission: Free

Opening hours: All day

How to get there: Drive yourself or join a tour


4. Hearst Castle

Introduction: Luxurious estates are not uncommon in California, but none can compare with Hearst Castle. During his lifetime, its owner was a media tycoon in the United States during the 1920s and 1930s. Perched by the sea and built against the mountains, the castle offers sweeping views of the blue ocean and sky. It has now become a distinctive landmark complex in the area.

Address: 50 Hearst Castle Road, San Simeon, CA, 93452

Admission: Adults $25, children $12

Opening hours: 08:00-16:00

How to get there: Drive yourself or join a tour

Official website: ***


Las Vegas - The Entertainment Capital


Introduction: Las Vegas, a magical city built in the desert, is known as the Entertainment Capital of the World and attracts nearly 40 million visitors each year. Its dazzling themed hotels, spectacular casinos, and superstar shows that keep the festivities going night after night have made this sleepless city in the desolate desert.

Recommended travel time: Year-round. If you do not like excessive heat, it is advisable to avoid June to August.

Attractions


Representative attractions:

1. Las Vegas Boulevard

Introduction: This is the most prosperous street in Las Vegas and the soul and symbol of the city. It is home to the most luxurious hotels, casinos, restaurants, shopping venues, and entertainment facilities. Eighteen of the world's top twenty-five hotels are located here.

Address: S Las Vegas Blvd, Las Vegas, NV 89109, USA

Admission: Free

Opening hours: All day

Transportation: If you would like to travel along the boulevard, you can take the bus for $2 per person per ride, or choose the monorail.


2. Red Rock Canyon National Conservation Area

Introduction: This is one of the most beautiful viewpoints in the Mojave Desert. It was formed by intense movements of seabed strata more than 600 million years ago. Today, the Mojave Desert's natural colors and uniquely shaped rocks combine perfectly to create a spectacular natural landscape of winding mountain ranges, layered peaks, and unusual summits.

Address: 1000 Scenic Loop Dr, Las Vegas, NV 89161, USA

Admission: $3 for motorcycles, bicycles, and pedestrians; $7 per vehicle

Opening hours: 6:00–19:00

How to get there: Drive yourself or join a tour

Official website: ***


3. High Roller Observation Wheel

Introduction: If you want to enjoy the beautiful scenery of Las Vegas, the High Roller Observation Wheel is sure to satisfy you! It is the largest observation wheel in the world, standing 30 meters taller than the London Eye in the UK. Its spacious glass cabins offer spectacular panoramic views. In just over ten minutes, you can reach the top of the wheel and overlook the entire city, experiencing the romantic, “city that never sleeps” charm of Las Vegas from above.

Address: 3545 S Las Vegas Blvd, Las Vegas, NV 89109, USA

Ticket prices: Daytime tickets cost $22 for adults and $9 for children. Nighttime tickets cost $32 for adults and $19 for children.

Opening hours: 11:30am-2:00am

Transportation: Take the Las Vegas Monorail and get off at Harrah's / The Linq Station or Flamingo / Caesars Palace Station.

Grand Canyon National Park


Features: Grand Canyon National Park is one of the Seven Natural Wonders of the World. Located in northwestern Arizona, it is divided into two main areas—the South Rim and the North Rim—each with its own unique features. The South Rim is the most visited area, and its scenic roads are exceptionally well maintained. The North Rim has a higher elevation and thinner air, but its skies are crystal clear. It is one of the national parks in the United States that is most worth visiting.

Recommended travel time: Spring and autumn offer cool temperatures and fewer visitors. Summer has the warmest temperatures and attracts more visitors.


Attractions


Featured attractions:

1. Hermit Road

Introduction: Hermit Road is an approximately 11-kilometer scenic route from the Hermit Rest Route Transfer to Hermits Rest. It features nearly all of the Grand Canyon's finest viewpoints and can be considered a route that connects the canyon's most classic grandeur and majesty. Visitors can take the park's Red Route shuttle bus to explore the nine viewpoints along the way.

Address: Grand Canyon Village, AZ 86023

Opening hours: Open all day

Transportation: Located within Grand Canyon National Park; taking the park shuttle bus is recommended.

Official website: ***


2. Desert View Drive

Introduction: Desert View Drive is a scenic highway that extends eastward along the canyon rim from Grand Canyon Village to Desert View Watchtower, covering a total distance of 40 kilometers. Along the way, you will find spectacular canyon views, picnic areas, the Tusayan Ruin Museum, the watchtower, and more, offering a different kind of breathtaking experience. If you are lucky, you may even see a California condor soaring overhead or a young deer quietly stopping by.

Address: Grand Canyon Village, AZ 86023

Opening hours: Open all day

Transportation: Self-driving or guided tour

Official website: ***


Yosemite National Park


Introduction: Yosemite is one of the most famous and popular national parks in the United States. Its sheer granite cliffs, vast ancient forests, abundant wildlife, and natural wonders make it a place visitors never want to leave.

Recommended travel period: April to June, when temperatures are comfortable and there are fewer visitors.


Attraction location


Featured attractions:

1. Yosemite Falls

Introduction: As its name suggests, Yosemite Falls is one of the park's signature sights. It is the most famous of the park's three major waterfalls and also the highest, with a total drop of 2,425 feet. It consists of three separate waterfall sections.

Address: Yosemite National Park, California

Opening hours: Open all day. Some roads may be closed in severe weather, so please check the official website for updates before traveling.

Transportation: Located within Yosemite National Park; taking the park shuttle is recommended.

Official website: ***


2. Horsetail Fall

Introduction: Horsetail Fall has a drop of 1,000 feet and flows from December to April of the following year. It is famous for the magical natural phenomenon known as the “Firefall.” This phenomenon occurs from mid-February to the end of February each year. When the weather is clear, the falling water reflects the orange sunlight at sunset, making it look from a distance as though the waterfall is on fire. The sight is spectacular.

Address: Yosemite National Park, California

Opening hours: Open all day. Some roads may be closed in severe weather, so please check the official website for updates before traveling.

Transportation: Located within Yosemite National Park; taking the park shuttle is recommended.

Official website: ***


3. Valley View

Introduction: Valley View offers one of Yosemite’s classic viewpoints and can be described as a postcard-perfect angle. Photography enthusiasts should not miss it. It is located along a relatively inconspicuous roadside, so you need to pay close attention or you may easily pass it by. However, once you arrive, you will find that the expansive and spectacular scenery is well worth seeking out. When visiting, search for “Valley View Yosemite” on Google Maps to find this beautiful photo spot more easily.

Address: Yosemite National Park, California

Opening hours: Open all day. In the event of severe weather, some roads may be closed. Please check the official website for notices before traveling.

Transportation: Located within Yosemite National Park. Taking the park shuttle is recommended.

Official attraction website: ***

Recommended Accommodation Areas in Each City


Los Angeles


When visiting Los Angeles, it is recommended to avoid the downtown and central city areas. Beverly Hills is a good choice, offering convenient transportation and location, as well as a safer and more pleasant environment. When visiting Disneyland, you can choose to stay in the nearby city of Anaheim or return to Los Angeles for accommodation.


San Francisco


San Francisco is the birthplace of boutique hotels, with a wide variety of uniquely styled rooms. Hotel prices are generally between $100 and $300, with overnight parking fees of $35–$50. Most tourists choose to stay near Union Square, which offers convenient transportation and relatively easy access to major attractions. A smaller number of visitors choose to stay near Fisherman’s Wharf, where prices are relatively higher but there are more dining options nearby.


Highway 1


Most accommodations with beautiful scenery and pleasant surroundings along Highway 1 are concentrated near Santa Barbara, Monterey, and the town of Carmel. These three towns are Pacific coastal cities in California, known for their beautiful scenery and suitability for relaxing vacations.


Las Vegas


Las Vegas is a sleepless city in a desolate desert. When visiting, you should of course stay along the lively and bustling Las Vegas Strip. The hotels here come in all styles and have their own distinctive features, including unique shows and buffets. Prices are also affordable, making Las Vegas one of the best places to experience five-star hotels.


Grand Canyon National Park


The Grand Canyon is located in Arizona. For visitors to the Grand Canyon, Tusayan is the preferred place to stay because of its relative convenience, but be sure to book accommodations well in advance. Some people also choose to stay in Las Vegas and take a day trip to the Grand Canyon by car.


Yosemite National Park


There are three main types of accommodation in Yosemite: hotels, cabins or tents, and camping with your own tent, for which a campsite must be reserved in advance. For hotels, staying in the Yosemite Valley area is recommended. It is located in the center of the park, where most attractions are concentrated and comprehensive facilities and services are available.
