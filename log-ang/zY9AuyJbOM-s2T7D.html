What is Burning Man?


Burning Man originated in the late 1800s, some artists, who in a place far from noise, built a city within one day, then burned it down within another day, using this way of creation and destruction to express art and self.

As a tradition, this festival was preserved. That's the current Burning Man.


Gradually, Burning Man gained more and more popularity among entrepreneurs, investors, and founders from listed companies.


Burning Man is very special—it's a carnival with tens of thousands of participants in the desert. Participants need to survive in the desert wilderness. They can witness spectacular desert art wonders, have a carnival with tens of thousands of playful people, and even spend a week with the world's top CEOs. It's full of both high challenges and temptations.


Why go to Burning Man?


Burning Man is the "utopia" that all art lovers yearn for.


Everyone who participates in Burning Man is called a Burner, and first-time participants are called "virgins". Everyone is a performer; here one must fully release themselves, touch art passionately with their entire life.


Modern art exhibitions, open-air concerts, building a dark city, eccentric costumes, extreme cosplay experiences, gift-giving, singles parties, weddings, self-expression... these are unique elements of Burning Man. Here, everyone is more like a "dream architect".


This challenging and artistic atmosphere attracts huge numbers of Silicon Valley geeks, such as Facebook founder Zuckerberg, famous entrepreneur Elon Musk, Google founders Brin and Page, and others~


In addition, Burning Man has various types of participants. Here you can find social scene regulars, tech nerds, mechanical artists, hippies, angry punks, internet millionaires, underground rebels, scholars, book editors, fire starters, and other explorers from all over the world.


The charm of Burning Man lies in how, when people step away from the flood of information and begin creating art, conversing with themselves, communicating authentically, helping one another, and truly listening - all these become things learned within this space.


Because here, there is no commerce; all supplies must be bartered in various forms - perhaps a dance, an embrace, a commemorative medal, or even a lock of hair.


When we sever all original social connections and release ourselves, only then can we truly enjoy the meaning Burning Man exists to provide.


When does Burning Man begin?


Burning Man is held between late August and early September each year. Tens of thousands gather in the Black Rock Desert of Nevada, USA, to create a brand new world that exists for only 8 days.


After those 8 days, all artworks will be burned, and this place returns to its original desert appearance - everything returns to reality.

The 2018 Burning Man festival will take place from August 26th through September 3rd.

The Ten Principles of Burning Man


1. Radical Inclusion — Unconditional Welcome

Anyone can be part of Burning Man; there are no prerequisites, respecting every "member"


2. Gifting — Giving Without Expectation

Promoting giving without expectation of return, rather than cash or equivalent exchange behaviors


3. Decommodification — Removing Commercial Value

Refusing advertising, sponsorship, and other activities with commercial purposes


4. Radical Self-Reliance — Being Your Own Source

Exploring one's own capabilities, self-sufficiency (except for ice and coffee which can be purchased, Burning Man participants must bring all other life supplies themselves)


5. Radical Self-Expression — Expressing Oneself

You have the right to express yourself through various means


6. Communal Effort — Community Spirit

Encouraging cooperation and protecting community networks.


7.Civic Responsibility —— Civic Responsibility

One must clarify their civic responsibilities during activities and comply with local laws.


8.Leaving no trace —— Leaving No Trace

Take away all trash after the event concludes.


9.Participation —— Participation

Anyone can participate in any activity; you can be either an actor or a spectator.


10. Immediacy —— Immediacy

Explore immediate experiences and fearlessly discover your inner self.


Burning Man Survival Guide


In Black Rock City, the greatest challenge is battling extreme weather conditions. This refers not only to the scorching sun during the day and cold nights, but also to dust storms, tornadoes, and thunderstorms that may strike at any moment.


Therefore, you must prepare:

1. One gallon of water per person per day

2. A reusable large-capacity water bottle

3. Sufficient food and beverages

4. First aid kit

5. High-SPF sunscreen and sunglasses

6. Warm clothing

7. Wear shoes that cover your entire foot (boots are best); do not prioritize comfort or convenience by wearing flip-flops or sandals that expose skin, as feet easily become dry and irritated

8. Dust mask (PM2.5 protection)

9. Wind-resistant glasses

10. Hand sanitizer

11. Trash bags and tools for cleaning your campsite

12. Waterproof tape (duct tape)

13. Flashlight/headlamp with spare batteries

14. Lighting equipment to wear on your person and attach to your bicycle at night

15. If you are not staying in an RV: a sturdy tent, warm sleeping bag, and moisture barrier pad.

(A sleeping bag must be warm enough because desert temperatures drop suddenly at night)

16. Common sense, open-mindedness, humor, and a positive attitude.


Burning Man Festival Important Notes


1. Leave No Trace is the most important rule for attending Burning Man. The Black Rock Desert has been an isolated national protected area for over 10,000 years, and every Burner has a responsibility to respect and protect this land so that this gathering can continue to be held here.


2. Protect your valuables. Black Rock City is not completely a utopia where nothing lost is ever found. When you leave your tent, especially on the night of burning the Giant, keep your valuables safe.


3. Respect personal boundaries and uphold consent principles. Black Rock City is full of sexual stimulation, especially for first-time attendees. So always maintain safety awareness in any situation.


4. Don't steal trail markers or damage other public facilities. There is no cell phone signal in Black Rock City; it relies entirely on maps and signs. Emergency services depend on signs to locate positions, and all participants need the signs to navigate, so all trail markers must remain in their original locations until the final day.


5. According to the principles of free gifting and de-commercialization, nothing can be bought or sold in Black Rock City, and no commercial activities can be conducted (such as advertising). There are no transactions here, only sharing and free exchange.

5. After payment is confirmed, wait for the official confirmation email.


Purchasing tickets requires time and effort. If you fail to secure a ticket, prefer not to invest personal energy in the process, decide late and miss the purchase window, or cannot participate in the sale, transferring tickets becomes your only option. Some travel platforms also offer ticket sales.


Where do people stay during Burning Man?


During the festival, accommodation options include applying to reside in a designated camp with tents or in an RV.


Registering for Camp


Applying for camp involves site selection considerations. Before applying for your camp, you need to prepare a camp layout plan. Camps are paid, and each camp typically has its own theme, organizing unique activities. Choose a camp based on your interests. Apply early to avoid missing out on camps you're interested in before they reach capacity!


RV vs. Regular Vehicle with Tent


Driving into Black Rock City involves either an RV or a regular vehicle plus tent


1. RV:

The living conditions in Black Rock Desert are extremely harsh; camping without an RV means you won't have a place to shower for the first 8 days, but RVs come with a shower room~

Besides that, there is air conditioning, bathroom, kitchen, and refrigerator. In such a harsh environment as Black Rock Desert, living conditions are relatively comfortable. The downside is it's expensive and hard to rent.


2. Regular Vehicle + Tent:

Some people choose driving and setting up tents. This is cheaper than an RV, but the disadvantage is poorer comfort. However, for friends who want to fully experience the challenge of Burning Man, this is also a good choice.
