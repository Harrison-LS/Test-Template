Tips:

Origin of the Tony Award


The Tony Award is the highest honor for American theater and musical theater, established in 1947. Along with the film Academy Awards (Oscars), music Grammy Awards, and television Emmy Awards, it's known as one of the "Four Major American Arts Awards."

The name of the Tony Award was also chosen to honor a theatrical artist whose name was Antonette Perry, nicknamed Tony. This artist not only won the Pulitzer Prize in Drama but also discovered many musical theater talents through plays, including Hollywood star James Stewart. To recognize Perry's long-term contributions to Broadway, the American Theatre Wing established the Tony Award two years after her death.

Secret Realm Exploration | National Park Hiking Experience American Summer Camp


The Rocky Mountain range is the longest mountain chain in North America, stretching from the northernmost part of British Columbia province in western Canada all the way to New Mexico. Rocky Mountain National Park in the United States is located in Colorado state, only about an hour's drive from downtown Denver.


Here mountains and lakes are abundant; more than 355 kilometers of scenic hiking trails wind through dense forests, making it a paradise for hiking enthusiasts. There are also many rare plants and animals. Late summer is the best time to hike in the Rockies: magnificent canyons, rolling mountain ranges... exploring nature's hidden treasures while enjoying the fun of hiking, there will always be a breathtaking view that leaves you awe-struck.


Tour Guide


Visiting Rocky Mountain National Park requires at least 3 days. There are many facilities-complete independent cabins and camping sites in the surrounding areas where you can experience typical American outdoor recreational life (summer camping is extremely popular; if you plan to camp within the park, it's best to reserve your spot in advance through the official website).


Rocky Mountain National Park features numerous hiking trails of different difficulty levels, centered around several beautiful lakes including Bear Lake in the central area, as well as Nymph Lake, Dream Lake, and Emerald Lake. Starting hikes from Bear Lake, trail markers are very clear along the way, and there are free shuttle buses within the park. During summer (July to September), visitors can also witness stunning glacial relics at Rocky Mountain Park.


Important considerations for hiking in the park


• The park's trails are situated at significant elevations with substantial elevation changes, potentially causing altitude-related symptoms. Hikers should research trail conditions beforehand and adjust their approach based on personal fitness levels;

• Even without feeling thirsty, thin mountain air rapidly depletes bodily fluids, making consistent hydration essential for a safe hiking experience;

• Sparse mountain atmosphere intensifies ultraviolet exposure, requiring robust sun protection strategies like sunglasses and long-sleeved clothing to safeguard against potential skin damage;

• Variable weather conditions and terrain dynamics necessitate checking the official website before embarking on any hiking journey to stay informed about current trail status.

Experience Nature | Cool Summer Vacation: Visit Famous Photo Spots at Northern Border


Summer in the US is always sunny and hot. Instead of trying to escape the heat, why not cool off near Niagara Falls at the northern border? This waterfall is one of North America's most renowned natural wonders. Standing on the observation deck, the Niagara River plunges dramatically from a cliff over 50 meters high, creating magnificent and wild whirlpools in the gorge. A simple photo captures the popular Instagram-worthy scenery, letting you experience both refreshing American summer vibes and northern natural beauty.


Thrilling Boat-Viewing Experience


In addition to experiencing the magnificent waterfall scenery at the viewing platform, travelers can also choose to take a "Maid of the Mist" boat tour to get up close with the waterfall. Within this 20-minute journey, feel the thrilling sensation as you are enveloped in water mist while the cruise ship slowly moves forward. Tourists can take out their cameras and capture Niagara Falls' fairy-tale-like scenery through the gentle mist. They can also shoot upward at the cascading waterfall when the boat approaches the falls, capturing the breathtaking views into their lenses.


Niagara Falls

--Niagara Falls is located at the border between Ontario, Canada and New York State, USA. It is one of the most famous natural wonders on the North American continent and one of the world's three largest international waterfalls.

--The Great Falls consist of three sections: Horseshoe Falls, American Falls, and Bridal Veil Falls. With an average flow rate of 5720 cubic meters per second, the thunderous roar creates a spectacular natural spectacle.

--Most of the waterfall faces Canada, offering excellent viewing angles from within Canadian territory. Visitors can explore through various activities: Hornblower Niagara Cruises, white-water walking trails, Mist Walker zipline adventures, behind-the-falls expeditions, and helicopter tours. Some experiences provide complimentary rain gear on-site for added comfort during your visit.

--Can be done as a day trip from downtown Toronto, or stay overnight to watch the light show. At night when colorful lights illuminate the majestic waterfall, it creates another beautiful scene. Every summer there are water fireworks shows twice weekly.

Golf basics before the tournament


The PGA Golf Tour Championship has nearly a century of history. Initially, golf was often controlled by wealthy amateurs, so the founders of PGA established this tournament to improve social status and influence for professional golf players. Last year's defending champion was Tiger Woods.

Since its founding in 1916, the PGA Championship has become one of the most prestigious world golf tournaments, and today it is one of the four major championships in golf.
