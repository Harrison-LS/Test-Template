San Francisco

Three nights in San Francisco, 2026

I traveled to San Francisco for my son’s university graduation. My brother flew in from Japan, and with beautiful weather throughout, it turned into a wonderful trip.

Because Hawaiian Airlines flies to San Francisco nonstop from the islands, Huntington's trip proved to be much simpler and more enjoyable.

Positive thoughts on the food served onboard: I loved them - and I mean, chocolate chicken pesto sandwiches are amazing.

After meeting my husband (who arrived first) and my son at the San Francisco airport at about 8:30 p.m., we went to have dinner at the hotel’s In-N-Out. My sports-loving brother was overjoyed because he learned it's one of Shohei Ohtani’s favorite places.

Although McDonald's combo meals are over $10 in Hawaii, In-N-Out burgers are of really good quality.

Perhaps it was pure hunger, but that may have been the best In-N-Out meal of all time. Even though it was after 9 p.m. and a weekday, the place was packed.

It was hard to decide on a hotel because of my brother's presence. We thought about Airbnbs and hotels in the center, but because my husband was sure of getting a rental, we chose a hotel by the airport. While I wasn’t so keen on it at first, the included breakfast, fast airport shuttle, and laundry facilities made it a wise choice. Sure the property was dated, but the room was huge.

The bathroom was huge. So was the bathtub.

While there appeared to be an old water stain on the wall, the tub was spotless. \ There was a microwave, coffee maker, and refrigerator in the room. \ There was also a surplus of hangers. \ The following morning, we explored the hotel further. The lobby was quite spacious as well. \ I had only anticipated a basic continental breakfast, but the buffet was far more impressive, offering several egg options, and, depending on the day, sausage and potatoes. \ They also had a waffle station along with yogurt, juice, and plenty more. \ Considering the high prices in San Francisco, it was a real bonus that breakfast was included with the room. \ The exterior of the hotel. \ On our last visit the weather had been terrible and freezing, but this time it was sunny. It was a bit cool, but very pleasant. \ The shuttle bus schedule. It also serves a few other hotels in the area. \ The laundry room was also a huge help. There was no competition for the machines. Washing and drying each took 30 minutes, and $2 per machine, which is quite reasonable. \ There was a gym too, which was another plus for my husband along with the free parking. \ Then it was time for a half day of sightseeing for my daughter and brother who were visiting San Francisco for the first time. My husband drove us to Union Square while the three of us went off on our own. \ This was my third visit to San Francisco but I had only been to Union Square on my first trip about 30 years ago. I had no memory of it at all.

I had heard deliberate danger and violence forced numerous retail stores to shut down, although Macy's and other high-end stores were still in business. Hopefully, the region is on the path to becoming stable again.

My daughter had to stop by the figurine store because nothing like it exists in Hawaii.

Then we were off to ride the cable car. Watching the car get turned around by hand at the terminus is part of the experience.

I wanted to stand outside and hold the bar, but I noticed it was only younger people doing that, so I abandoned the idea.

Our cable car.

We were lucky to get seats facing outward.

The views were breathtaking and the streets of San Francisco are so stylish, the ride was a blast.

We got off at the terminal and headed to our next destination. The hills in San Francisco are tough to walk up but they are what gives the city it's charm.

Even the fire trucks were retro and stylish.

Gorgeous.

We reached Lombard Street, one of my favorite locations in the city. I had hoped to go all the way to the top and then walk down, but the steep streets on the way there had worn me out, so I had to forfeit the idea. Reminder that I need to exercise more.

The sunny weather made it even prettier.

My husband was waiting for us downtown so from there we finally decided to take a self-driving Waymo. Although the photos are not spectacular, the ride experience was really good. The ride was smooth and I never felt nervous. We compared the price with a regular rideshare, and Waymo actually cost a little more. However, being the first driverless ride from Waymo was a unique experience, so we appreciated it. When we exited the vehicle, we took a bit too much time and caused a small traffic jam. The car behind us was also a Waymo, so it was really patient.

Then we went to Fisherman's Wharf

I remembered the last time we visited, I ate at the restaurant next door, but it had since closed down and moved, so we went to this one instead.

We got the classic clam chowder served in a sourdough bread bowl and fried calamari. The portions were generous and the food was decent.

From a distance, we could see Alcatraz Island, but the weather was perfect for a walk, so we enjoyed it.

The actual purpose of this visit was...... the sea lions. My daughter adores animals, so even though it was pretty chilly next to the water, she spent a great deal of time watching them and was in heaven. It was nice that she was so happy.

On the return ride to the hotel, we saw a historic and grand building, and we were curious what it was. It turned out to be City Hall, and after seeing it, we realized the one in Hawaii is much smaller in comparison.

We were tired, so we took a long rest before going out to get souvenirs. I forgot my camera, but we made a stop at a Trader Joe's. They have great, unique food as well as good prices which means it’s great for souvenir shopping!



For dinner, we went to a Chinese restaurant that was right next to the hotel. It had a nice view because it was on the water.

There were a lot of Indian and Chinese restaurants in San Francisco. I guess all of the competition must keep the quality high. The stir fry with pork, eggplant, and green beans was amazing.

So was the tofu and squid. We also had fried rice and chow fun and all of it was great.

The next day was graduation day. The breakfast room was so crowded that morning that they set up some tables and chairs on the outdoor terrace.

I thought it was a nice bonus to have breakfast while watching the planes take off and land.

Everyone went to my son's college first. The weather there was a lot cloudier than by the hotel and it was really cold. We got a true San Francisco experience.

Like last time, we ate in the cafeteria which I really enjoy.

They have a great buffet with a really good salad bar that is fully stocked with vegetables, a bunch of options for the main course, desserts, and drinks. You can't really ask for much more.

Then we went to the bookstore and bought some cold weather gear with the college logo on it. My brother loved having a nice souvenir and was surprised by how big an American university is.

After that, we took graduation photos around the campus in caps and gowns. There were photo sessions going on everywhere.

Friends also joined us. Congratulations to all!

Everyone boarded the train to the venue.

Here it comes.

The ceremony took place in a baseball stadium.

Oracle Park was a special visit for my sports crazy brother. He stated that American ballparks are very beautiful.

There was a large number of attendees.

And a huge number of graduates as well — over 7,000.

He’s super hard to see, right? He was hard to see at all with that massive number of graduates.

While taking pictures with graduates, we were all parted by the crowd. It was pleasing to see my son surrounded by so many good friends as a parent. Congratulations son!

Our family accompanied my son’s girlfriend to a celebratory dinner.

It was to an Italian restaurant my son had wanted to try.

The place had nice vibes and I had a delicious mushroom risotto even though I did not take many pictures of it.

We had a long day and it was nice and deserved. We were so tired but it was an awesome day. We arrived back at the hotel past 11 p.m. after taking my son and the others to their hotel and after I collected something from the Waymo that my daughter forgot.

My brother departed first the following day. My daughter and I proceeded to the airport for our flight to Las Vegas. I made an expensive mistake at the airport. I miscalculated my suitcase thinking it was within the low-cost airline’s carry-on size limits. I had to pay an extra $79 in checked-baggage fees because the wheels were too big. I could have cried if I hadn’t measured it with a tape measure.

I chose to ignore it for now, and keep hoping I’d have better luck in Las Vegas.

With it being Memorial Day Weekend, we showed up to the airport ready for the anticipated traffic, and long lines; but we were pleasantly surprised to find San Francisco International Airport to be quite the enjoyable wait.

One of the things that made the long wait enjoyable was the food.

They sell pre-packaged and airplane style heated meals, which we found to be very tasty and healthy. All meals also seemed to be a decent enough price that we would recommend it to others.

Two of the souvenir shops we visited also had really cute things that I’d consider to be decent quality items.

San Francisco was an enjoyable experience.
