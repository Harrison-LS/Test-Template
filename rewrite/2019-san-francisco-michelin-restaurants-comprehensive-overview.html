2019 San Francisco Michelin Restaurants Comprehensive Overview

Samsung Restaurant

San Francisco is year-round hailed as the foodie capital, home to some of the world's finest restaurants. This year, several San Francisco restaurants once again earned prestigious Michelin star certifications. Quickly follow Director to get acquainted with each one:


Three-Star Restaurants


Atelier Crenn


Address: 3127 Fillmore St


Atelier Crenn


Atelier Crenn is an extremely artistic restaurant. Before dining, each guest receives a poem, with every line representing a dish.


Atelier Crenn


The chef at this restaurant, Dominique Crenn, was the first female chef to earn three Michelin stars.

She has stayed in many countries across Europe and Asia, so she is very skilled at blending various flavors together to give you a brand new dining experience.


Atelier Crenn


Benu


Address: 22 Hawthorne St.


Benu


This restaurant specializes in Asian flavors, very suitable for those with Chinese palates. Chef Corey Lee customizes different menus every night, giving you absolute surprises.


Benu


The signature dishes here include white sturgeon caviar and fresh sea urchin, seafood lovers will definitely enjoy them.

At the same time, his home's interior decoration is also quite unique; the earth-tone color scheme and exquisite detail design are as impeccable as his dishes.


Benu


Quince


Address: 470 Pacific Ave.


Quince


Located in Jackson Square, Quince is famous for its native Californian-style dishes. This restaurant is situated in a historic, charming landmark building and is headed by celebrity chef Michael Tusk.


Quince


He is very skilled at cooking exquisite delicacies using fresh seasonal ingredients.

Here every night there are two menus to choose from, along with complementary wines, guaranteed to leave you fully satisfied. Lazy Bear


Address: 3416 19th St.


Lazy Bear


Lazy Bear is probably one of the most unique dining experiences in San Francisco. Diners are seated at two long tables, and food is prepared right before their eyes in an open kitchen and then served.


Lazy Bear


Chef David Barzelay wants to create a theatrical experience for his guests, so he has waitstaff demonstrate every behind-the-scenes detail of the dishes' preparation.

Curious? Go explore quickly!


Lazy Bear


One-star restaurant


Angler


Address: 132 The Embarcadero


This restaurant creates its menu based on freshly caught seafood each day, serving you the freshest and most delicious taste.


Angler


His cooking methods are also quite unique; all ingredients are cooked on a large wood-fired stove, preserving the food's original flavors while adding a unique fragrant aroma. Additionally, it is located right next to the inner harbor pier, allowing you to satisfy both your taste buds and eyes while enjoying delicious food.


Angler


Hashiri


Address: 4 Mint Pl.


For those who love Japanese cuisine, you absolutely cannot miss Hashiri. This restaurant perfectly embodies the Tokyo Dogenzaka style, seamlessly blending classical and modern elements.


Hashiri


At the same time, they are meticulous about the quality of their dishes, perfectly combining traditional Japanese flavors with local specialties, giving you the most unique taste experience.


Hashiri


In Situ


Address: SFMOMA, 151 3rd St.


In Situ


In Situ, located within the San Francisco Museum of Modern Art, is committed to pushing the combination of food and art to its limits.

The dishes here not only satisfy your taste buds but also provide you with a visual feast.


San Francisco Museum of Modern Art

The San Francisco Museum of Modern Art is the first museum dedicated solely to collecting modern art on America's West Coast. One of the most distinctive features of this museum is its tall cylindrical zebra-striped skylight, through which light shines directly from the roof down to the basement floor, providing excellent natural lighting. Its innovative exterior design not only embodies San Francisco's artistic spirit but has also become a target for photography enthusiasts around the world. The museum houses works by 20th-century representative Western artists such as Henri Matisse (a Fauvist French painter), Andy Warhol (an American Pop Art painter), Frida Kahlo (a Mexican female painter), and others.

The San Francisco Contemporary Art Museum is a branch of New York's MoMA, slightly smaller in scale but still full of highlights. Especially if you happen to attend some special exhibitions, you've got yourself a treat!


In Situ


Many dishes are inspired by the collections within the museum—come and immerse yourself in art!


In Situ
