Rustic Charm with Luxurious Experience—Napa Valley


Napa Valley is famous for its numerous world-renowned wineries and is also hailed as an "adults' theme park." Besides the fine wines, this area also features a series of Michelin-starred restaurants, such as The Restaurant at Meadowood.

Pet-friendly and family-friendly lodging option - Mendocino County


Mendocino County is just a 2-hour drive from San Francisco, making it an excellent destination for families to enjoy activities and relaxation. Beyond wine tasting, consider hiking to explore the beautiful natural scenery surrounding the area, or try outdoor sports like biking or kayaking. Additionally, Mendocino County boasts more than 60 regional parks and national parks, along with locally unique museums and galleries.
