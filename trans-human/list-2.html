Los Angeles

Los Angeles is located on the west coast of the United States, with a city proper population of approximately 3.8 million people, making it the second-largest city in the US (after New York). It has a typical Mediterranean climate with minimal temperature variation throughout the year, rainy winters and dry summers; spring and autumn are best for tourism. The English name is Los Angeles, abbreviated as LA. The official nickname is "City of Angels," while the colloquial term is Sank Village.
