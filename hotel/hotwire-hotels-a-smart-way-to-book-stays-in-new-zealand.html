Hotwire Hotels: A Smart Way to Book Stays in New Zealand

Hotwire Hotels Are Practical For New Zealand Travel - But Only For Certain Kinds of Trips

When traveling to New Zealand, especially if you are on a budget, you need to spend a lot on accommodations. That’s where Hotwire hotels come in useful. The whole system is very user friendly. Basically, you get to pay for your hotel for a lower price because you accept the trade-off of less information. Sometimes you do not even get to find out the name of the hotel until after you have confirmed the reservation. That trade-off can be very useful, but it doesn’t just mean you save money as long as you book with a low price.

I would be inclined to say that Hotwire Hotels are best used for more tactical bookings and not just when you are planning to book the entire New Zealand trip. They work best for people who have other bookings for flexible travel, short distance stays and don’t have very rigid travel plans. They are not ideal for people who are most concerned about where they get to stay that assures their trip plans, cancellation policies, parking, room configurations and if the lodging is accessible for certain people and travel plans that have specific timings.

There is a lot of travel advice that says if something is the cheapest option, you take it. I would say that barely passes for advice. In New Zealand, where travelers go from cities to small towns to the countryside for activities and scenic drives and are constantly on the move, you can’t simply say that a hotel is the cheapest, so you take it. It can be more expensive and mean a lot of logistical problems like stress, plan failure, and a lot of time wasted.

How Hotwire Hotels Change The Booking Process

Usually booking a hotel means you get to see everything about the hotel including the name and address, room types, reviews, and reservation policies prior to buying. Hotwire hotels change the process of booking travel. Instead of picking a hotel by location, travellers can choose a deal based off the information provided: general area, star rating/quality rating, guest rating, amenities, room type, and booking restrictions. This model can provide real value because some hotels do not publicize each room discounted under their name. If travellers accept the uncertainty that comes with booking this way, they may get a discounted rate lower than what is available to the public. However, it should be noted that uncertainty is the product being offered. For those who need certainty, wishing for this after booking will not change the outcome. The proper way to frame the question is not “Is this a Hotwire hotel deal?” Rather, the question is, “Would this booking still be a good deal if the final hotel were a property in the least appealing position as described in the listing?” This is especially the case when booking hotels in New Zealand. A hotel deal description stating the hotel property is located within a general area can be very misleading. It could be far from your pick-up location(intercity coach, car rental, flight,) the downtown/waterfront area, an event location, or your hotel if you have an early departure planned. A cheaper room deal is no deal at all if it means you are stuck with an inconvenient transfer or puts you farther from the downtown/city area. # New Zealand Has Unique Challenges for Travel New Zealand travel is characterized by a high degree of movement. Most itineraries comprise a week in a single neighborhood; they combine arrival cities with stops and overnight stays along a longer route. As a result, the selection of hotels along a route becomes more significant than it would be for a quick weekend city break.

For instance, a traveler staying a few nights in a big city might be ok with a stay in a unfamiliar hotel that is located in a sufficient neighborhood. They have the ability to change some things (take public transport, pick dining and activities that are nearby, etc.) once they get to the city. In the meantime, a traveler with a single overnight stay before a super early morning flight, a long ferry ride, an organized excursion, or a long driving trip has very little room for error. In that case, it is better to pay the discounted rate and be 100% certain about where you will be staying.

This does not mean that Hotwire Hotels are bad for New Zealand. It means that they should be used in the context of the type of trip that you will be taking. The more detailed your travel itinerary is (i.e. the more complicated), the more you should avoid opaquely booking hotels. The less detailed your stay is, the more flexibility you have.

Travelers often think that a hotel's category is the be all end all, but that is not always true. It is possible to have a nice hotel in a nice location that would work well for what you are trying to do, but you may end up staying in a hotel that is not very well located. You should also look at reviews and ratings, but those are not substitutes for actually knowing where you will end up sleeping.

When Booking Hotwire Hotels Makes Sense

Hotwire hotels make sense when you save a good amount of money and the risk is minimal. Several situations fit that description.

A flexible, leisure stay in a familiar or compact area

You might want to consider a Hotwire deal if you are going to be in a major destination for a few nights and you will be comfortable staying in a zone rather than at an exact spot. You have to be honest with yourself with the zone. Treating a broader area label as if it is certain the hotel will be near the attractions, the station or the restaurants is a fool’s errand.

You also have to look at the map of the destination, you have to consider the size of the area and if you would be okay if the hotel was located at the edge of the area. If you don’t mind if the hotel is not located near specific destinations, then this deal could work for your vacation. If you need to be a short distance away from a specific spot, book a hotel that has a name.

A last minute stay with no special requests

Many travelers who are booking really close to their arrival discover Hotwire hotels and like that they can book a hotel with no preference to the specific brand or hotel. When you make a last minute decision, the trade off is easier, you are likely booking what is available, compared to the months it can take a traveler to plan the perfect getaway.

A single overnight stop on a flexible road trip For self-drive travelers and their vehicles with a vague itinerary, an opaque hotel can work for a quick overnight stop. In such cases, guests are likely looking for an inexpensive and comfortable place to rest, rather than a hotel that defines the trip. Parking should be a major consideration, rather than an after thought when booking an opaque hotel. You may be thinking you found a good bargain on a cheap room, but a lower, more affordable rate may be offsetting the cost of paid or limited parking, and an even longer distance to an even more inconvenient parking spot. If convenient parking is essential, do not book until the listing offers a satisfactory level of reassurance that the parking situation will not present a problem for you. A solo or couple’s trip with simple room needs Hotwire hotels may be useful for situations where the room requirement is fairly simple. An adult or two that can share a bed has more flexibility than a family, larger group or adult travelers needing a separate beds. Pulley beds, connecting rooms, a crib, a kitchen, etc. usually require booking a room with a name. When the lowest Hotwire rate should not be your choice There are many cases that warrant the choice to spend more for a greater sense of assurance. This is not a defeatist point of view, it is logically focused trip planning. Arrival and departure nights The nights on either end of a long travel night are not the best time to be taking chances. When travellers first arrive, they are often navigating jet lag, heavy luggage, and managing a rental car. Conversely, when travellers return, they may need convenient transportation to the airport or terminal, luggage storage, and convenient airport parking. For these instances, booking a hotel with a confirmed address provides higher value than a discounted booking. This especially applies to bookings associated with a transport connection. The agony of waking up early and planning to check out to make a connecting train or flight could easily be eliminated with certainty. Jumping at Hotwire’s discounts for families should be avoided. Rooms are often booked with limited information about room capacity. Families with children will likely need a room with laundry and kitchen facilities, as well as proximity to lower daily transport demand. Groups will likely need adjacent rooms with separate beds and reliable shared spaces. A seemingly cheap room may actually create an expensive inconvenience for families. Uncertainty around distance to activities and transport may be worse than the potential for savings. If your itinerary centers around a series of connected activities, venues, or offices (conference/meeting spot, wedding, hospital, university, main attraction or trail, rental car location, transport connection), do not take an opaque deal since the closest location may not be convenient. Location can vary on a map due to different factors like roads, the terrain, weather, luggage, mobility, and available transport. The suggestion to take a taxi or rideshare isn’t as good of an answer as some people think. Each additional transfer increases the price of the trip and decreases flexibility. The value ofAccuratelyNamedHotel may be higher when its room price is higher. Any booking where flexibility is critical Uncertainty is not something that can be planned for. Restriction causes concern if flexibility is priority. Booking cancellable is better than having non-refundable hotel terms. A low price doesn’t always mean a low risk. A low price may mean that the guest is taking on a financial risk. Read the terms and conditions before confirming a Hotwire hotel to make sure you are not taking on too much risk. The terms of the listing may vary, so do not assume the terms of the listing apply to you. The key to evaluating a Hotwire Hotel properly is making sound decisions rather than trying to guess the hidden hotel. Hotwire users should make sound decisions based on the information provided rather than trying to discern hotel amenities, property photos, or reviews.” Hotels offer a range of amenities and room types as they update and improve their facilities. Some online information may be outdated or incomplete.

Focus on the information that is displayed.

  1. Start with the area. Open a map and determine the boundaries of the area specified. Think about arriving to the location in the morning and leaving in the evening, not just visiting for a short period of time.
  2. Read room descriptions. Verify the sleeping capacity along with bedding description and ensure that the description fulfills your needs. Be cautious not to assume a preference for a bed type, view, or configuration that is not described.
  3. Prioritize the amenities offered. Different travel itineraries may have varying priorities for amenities. Examples include Wi-Fi, parking, air conditioning, breakfast, a kitchen, laundry, accessibility amenities, a gym, and/or an on-site restaurant.
  4. Review the booking summary. Ensure the final booking amount reflects the stated rate for the selected booking dates.
  5. Review the cancellation policy and change fees. This is not the fine print for the booking. Describe what is offered with the booking along with the flexibility for cancellations and changes.
  6. Think of the pricing difference along with the value for certainty. If a hotel is named, and there is a difference in price for the booking compared to what is offered at a lower price, the booked hotel that has the named booking may be the best deal.
    7. This process may sound not as thrilling as searching for secret discounts but it is how you make the most out of your accidental savings. The objective is not about winning the booking competition but defending the trip. Don't compare apples to oranges, or more appropriately, to wishful thinking. One of the most common mistakes when evaluating Hotwire deals, for instance, is to compare prices against the best flexible rate at the most recognizable hotel, in order to ultimately claim that it's a great deal. This can easily manipulate your thinking and make you believe you've scored a great deal. To avoid this, compare Hotwire's prepaid, non-cancelable reservation and a similar prepaid, non-cancelable booking at a similar hotel. Once you understand the price difference relative to the flexible bookings of named hotels, you can decide if the comfort of flexibility is worth the additional expense. When comparing accommodations, amenities are important. Some hotels may try to impress you with amenities you may not even use. A hotel's pool is not a bargain if you are going to be out all day. Neither is a hotel's breakfast is you are going to leave before the breakfast is even served. Parking is worthless if you are not going to use it and potentially costs you more. A simple amenity like laundry or a kitchenette can be useful on a longer New Zealand trip, even if the hotel is not as nice.

    Booking in NZ requires some flexibility. Use Hotwire for more flexible bookings and name the hotel for more important bookings. This approach is better than using Hotwire exclusively. Use named hotels for the important bookings, like the first or last nights of travel, family trips, transport connections, or stops that are remote or logistically difficult. For the less important bookings, leave the names to Hotwire. This way, you can save money on more than just a few bookings. There's an easy rule that people can follow: Only book a Hotwire hotel if the price savings justify the downgrade. If the price difference is small, book the hotel with a name. The room will likely be cleaner, the address will be known, and there will be more flexibility. For most people, these factors will improve a trip. Using Hotwire is a smart choice for bookings where the price is low, and the offer fits your needs. However, Hotwire is only good as the deal. A traveler can say, “I know the area is acceptable, I can use the room as described, I accept the booking terms, and the total cost is meaningfully better than satisfactory alternatives.” When any of those are untrue, continue the search.

    Final Verdict: Use Hotwire Hotels With Discipline

    Hotwire hotels are useful to get lower prices on hotels in New Zealand, but plenty of users have found better deals elsewhere because of the compromises required. When you use Hotwire, you give up identity of the hotel prior to booking, you give up some location control, and you restrict your flexibility. If you book with Hotwire, you may walk away with a more substantial budget for activities or even more nights away, but you may walk away with more nights unbooked, due to limited flexibility. That is a valid trade. It's not a universal one. Travelers don't book Hotwire hotels because they think they scored a great deal and compromised flexibility. Travelers book Hotwire hotels because the total cost of the hotel is lower than alternatives and because the disclosed details match their travel needs. Hotwire hotels are useful tricks if you need flexibility, but if you have a binding travel itinerary, you should pay for certainty. New Zealand has a lot of different overnight destinations and Hotwire hotels are useful only if you control your use of the program. Because they are flexible, Hotwire hotels become truly valuable in New Zealand.
