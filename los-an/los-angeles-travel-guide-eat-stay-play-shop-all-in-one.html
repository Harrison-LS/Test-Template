Los Angeles Travel Guide | Eat, Stay, Play, Shop – All in One!

Los Angeles is a must-visit city in western America, featuring iconic landmarks, theme parks, sun-drenched beaches, and trendy new spots, yet often overlooked as merely a transit stop. If you have extra time, I recommend staying here. Follow this guide for authentic Los Angeles experiences covering dining, accommodation, entertainment, and shopping!


Pre-Trip Guide


Why Choose Los Angeles


As one of the most popular tourist cities on America's West Coast, Los Angeles offers extremely rich tourism resources with numerous attractions. The Hollywood area, home to top-tier pop culture, showcases the charm of this fashion capital; the vibrant West Coast beaches provide wonderful opportunities for water activities; diverse theme parks allow visitors to escape from busy reality; plus emerging trendy landmarks and colorful art museums make it suitable for tourists of all preferences and ages.


Best Season Choice


Los Angeles is a city that can be visited any month, as it maintains mild temperatures year-round with distinct seasons. Approximately 300 days annually feature sunshine under its Mediterranean climate, so sun protection is essential. For those avoiding extreme heat, June through August should be avoided, though this period offers ideal water activity conditions with the longest daylight hours.


Transportation Options


Self-Driving: Although Los Angeles ranks as America's second-largest city, public transportation remains inadequate. Renting a car for self-driving provides the most convenient exploration method. Western US parking facilities are both more abundant and spacious compared to eastern regions.

Uber: Beyond self-driving, app-based ride-hailing services offer the most practical and cost-effective transportation solution.

Metro: Metro is the general term for Los Angeles public transportation, consisting of subway, light rail, and buses. There are 6 lines in total for subway and light rail, with the Red Line and Light Blue Line being most commonly used. The one-way fare is $1.75.


Consumer Information


Tax rates are relatively high at around 9.5%, and no tax refund is available except at duty-free stores.


A Visual Guide to the Real Los Angeles


The Los Angeles people imagine is typically central downtown Los Angeles, that is, the area marked Los Angeles on maps. But in practice, Los Angeles generally refers to Los Angeles County, that is, Los Angeles County itself. This region is very large, containing 88 cities, such as Beverly Hills, Santa Monica, Long Beach, and downtown Los Angeles - all belong to Los Angeles County. Furthermore, when we orally say going to visit Los Angeles, it generally refers to the Greater Los Angeles Area, that is, the area including surrounding Orange County, Riverside County, etc., 5 counties in total. Some famous attractions are located here, such as Disney Resort and Spa at Orange County, Desert Outlets at Riverside County, etc.


Must-Visit Attractions in Los Angeles


Hollywood Universal Studios


Introduction: Universal Studios Hollywood is an original movie-themed amusement park located on the grounds of a film and television studio in the northwest suburb of downtown Los Angeles, making it a must-visit destination for tourists visiting Los Angeles. In addition to attractions and sites related to movies and the Harry Potter Wizarding World, there are tram tours that will take you through behind-the-scenes techniques at Universal Studios production studios and some real filming locations.

Address: 100 Universal City Plaza, Universal City, CA 91608, USA

Ticket prices: Regular ticket $109-$129, Express Pass (skip-the-line) $179-$259

Opening hours: 10:00-18:00. The park's operating hours vary daily and may be adjusted earlier or later due to special events at the park. Please refer to the official website for accurate information.

Transportation: Take the red line subway to Universal City station, then exit. A free shuttle service is available nearby, just a 2-3 minute ride to Universal Studios.

California Disneyland


Introduction: Located in Anaheim, Disneyland Park is the world's first Disney theme park and the only one built under Walt Disney's direct supervision, filled with traditional Disney charm. Within this themed park, you will experience more dreamy and interesting experiences rather than thrilling adventures.

Address: 1313 Disneyland Dr, Anaheim, CA 92802, USA

Ticket prices: Single day adult ticket $104, child ticket $98. Two-day adult ticket $225, child ticket $210. One-day two-park ticket $154.

Opening hours: 08:00-00:00 next day (California Disneyland), 08:00-22:00 (Disney California Adventure). Each park's opening hours vary by season, please check the official website for specifics.

Transportation: Take buses 43, 83, 430, or 460 and get off at Disneyland station.

              Getty Center


Introduction: The Getty Center is an important landmark cultural site in Los Angeles, and one of the richest museums in California or even worldwide for its extensive collections, a destination artists seek. Here are collected vast amounts of European paintings, sculptures, decorative arts, and photography works. Not only that, the architecture itself of the Getty Center is also a work of art; visitors need to take a small train up the hill, while enjoying views of the entire valley scenery. Stepping out of the museum is the colorful Getty Central Garden, its reputation not inferior to artworks inside the museum, tree-shaded paths, gentle flowing water, streams along the path merging into ponds, in the center of the pond lies an azalea maze, everywhere embodying artistic atmosphere.

Address: 1200 Getty Center Dr, Los Angeles, CA 90049

Ticket prices: Free

Opening hours: Tuesday-Friday and Sunday 10:00-17:30, Saturday 10:00-21:00, closed on Mondays

Transportation: Can drive or take bus 234 at Sepulveda / Getty Center station. Visitors can only park cars in the parking lot at the foot of the hill, then take a cable car round trip between the summit arts center.

OUE Skyspace Observation Deck


Introduction: OUE Skyspace is California's highest open-air observation deck, located within the Bank of America Tower in downtown Los Angeles, more than 1,000 feet above ground level, offering a 360-degree panoramic view of "The City of Angels." Here, you can not only sit quietly on a small window-side sofa indoors to enjoy the sunset in peace, but also step outside for an intimate encounter with the surrounding skyscrapers. The outdoor observation deck is surrounded by floor-to-ceiling transparent glass walls, providing excellent visibility. It features viral attractions including a 70-story suspended transparent slide and angel wings photo spots, making it a must-visit new landmark when in Los Angeles.

Address: 633 W 5th St #840, Los Angeles, CA 90071

Ticket Prices: Admission for adults $25, children $19. Ticket + slide combo package for adults $33, children $27.

Opening Hours: 10:00-22:00

Transportation: Take buses #16, 17, 18, 53, 55, 62, 316, or 355 to the 5th&Grand stop. A one-minute walk will bring you here.

Official Website: ***


Griffith Observatory


Introduction: This is one of the iconic buildings in Los Angeles, located on a mountain northwest of the city center, offering panoramic views of the entire city of Los Angeles. Not only that, it is also one of the world's famous astronomical observatories and countless Hollywood movie filming locations. Here you can not only enter the observatory to learn about astronomy knowledge of planets and the universe, or watch planetarium shows demonstrating celestial movements, but also walk up to the outdoor viewing deck for a distant view of downtown Los Angeles and a panoramic view of Hollywood.

Address: 2800 E Observatory Rd, Los Angeles, CA 90027

Ticket Price: Free

Opening Hours: Tuesday-Friday 12:00-10:00pm, weekends 10:00am-10:00pm, closed on Mondays.

Transportation: Take the DASH Weekend Observatory Shuttle from downtown and get off at Griffith Observatory station.

Official Website: ***


Six Flags Magic Mountain


Introduction: Los Angeles offers more than just charming beaches and art exhibitions—there is the most thrilling Six Flags Magic Mountain. Located in Valencia, northern Los Angeles, established in 1971, this park ranks as having the most roller coasters globally. To date, nearly 20 roller coasters within the park have won world awards, including Green Lantern (the world's first 4D coaster), Goliath (longest coaster in America), Tower of Terror (second highest drop tower worldwide), and Big Apple Spinning Coaster (America's longest 360° loop coaster). It is truly a paradise for adventure enthusiasts!

Address: 26101 Magic Mountain Pkwy, Valencia, CA 91355

Ticket Prices: Adults $72.99, Children $47.99

Operating Hours: Opens at 10:30 AM; typically closes between 8:00 PM and 9:00 PM; please refer to the official website for specific times.

Transportation: By car, or by taking Metro Red Line to North Hollywood station, transferring to North Hollywood Route 757 express bus to Regional Transit (MRTC) station, then taking Santa Clarita Routes 3 or 7 to Six Flags Magic Mountain.

Park Website: ***


Hollywood Walk of Fame


Introduction: The Hollywood Walk of Fame was built in 1958, is a sidewalk extending along Hollywood Boulevard and Vine Street in the USA, a famous cultural historical landmark of Los Angeles. There are more than 2000 star-shaped medals with names of Hollywood celebrities on the street, commemorating their contributions to the entertainment industry. The famous Madame Tussauds Wax Museum, Chinese Theater, and Kodak Theatre are located on this boulevard.

Address: 6901 Hollywood Blvd, Los Angeles, CA 90028, USA

Ticket prices: Free

Opening hours: All day

Transportation: Take bus routes 212, 217, or 312 and get off at Hollywood / Highland station.

Accommodation Recommendations


Los Angeles is very large, and public transportation cannot reach every corner, so choosing the location of accommodation is especially important. If you want to experience coastal life, it is recommended to stay near Santa Monica. Fashion enthusiasts and those who love unique hotels are advised to choose around Beverly Hills.


Beverly Hills


Introduction: This area has many attractions concentrated together, with a wide variety of shopping and dining options, excellent transportation, and is the host venue for the annual Oscars. Although Beverly Hills is a famous wealthy district, hotel prices are not expensive. The surrounding accommodation environment is mature and safe, with everything from convenient hotels to ultimate luxury available.

Representative hotel: The Beverly Hills Hotel

This hotel that bursts with girly charm is known by Californians as the pink palace. If you're a social media enthusiast, you've definitely seen its viral photos. The entire hotel is surrounded by lush greenery on 12 acres of vibrant trees. Striped lounge chairs, a blue swimming pool, a romantic coffee shop, and palm trees covering the exterior walls - every detail comes together perfectly. This must be where influencer hotels originated from. Marilyn Monroe once stayed here for three consecutive months. The hotel is not only visually stunning but also provides excellent service. Luxury car pickup and drop-off services are available within 3 kilometers with drivers included, just like scenes in American movies. It's truly cool beyond measure.


Korean Town


Introduction: Located in Los Angeles midtown, although there aren't many attractions, the hotel prices are more affordable. Not far west are Beverly Hills and Santa Monica, not far east is downtown, and not far north is Hollywood. The distances are similar, making it very suitable for tourists who prefer self-driving. Moreover, Korean Town offers a wide variety of food choices with operating hours extending late into the night.

Representative hotel: The LINE Hotel

An industrial-style influencer hotel created by Korean designers. Industrial minimalist style, and the coffee shop within the hotel is also very famous. Convenient location with an elegant environment. A subway station is right at the entrance, making it easy to get anywhere.


Santa Monica


Introduction: This is the most popular beach area in the local region, perfect for tourists who want to enjoy vacation and seaside life.

Representative hotel: Shore Hotel

The hotel is in an excellent location, directly opposite Santa Monica Beach and close to Pacific Park. Restaurants line both sides of the property, with shopping malls nearby—very convenient. Every room features a private balcony, and ocean view rooms offer breathtaking views of the endless Pacific Ocean.


Local Specialty Food Recommendations


American Fast Food


In-N-Out Burger

In-N-Out Burger is one of the most popular chain burger restaurants on the West Coast. All their beef is fresh and never frozen, with a simple menu offering just three common burgers. However, their secret menu can satisfy any craving—healthy lettuce-wrapped burgers are available too. Fries and milkshakes are also signature products.


Shake Shack

If there is In-N-Out Burger on the West Coast, then Shake Shack represents the East Coast. Some say that Lady Liberty, Empire State Building, and Shake Shack burgers are known as New York's three landmarks—demonstrating its status and importance. You can also enjoy this signature New York burger chain in Los Angeles. Highly recommended for their burgers, milkshakes, and fries.


Chick-Fil-A

This fast food chain is not as well-known domestically as the first two, but it is number one in American hearts, with revenue even exceeding McDonald's. It is a burger place that sells only chicken products. If you love fried chicken burgers, you must not miss this! It has ranked #1 for two consecutive years in Fast Casual magazine's annual best fast food contest in America.

Shopping Spree Recommendations


Rodeo Drive - Beverly Hills


Regarded as "one of the world's top ten shopping paradises", international luxury jewelry and fashion from renowned masters are gathered here. The lavish storefronts resemble art galleries, touted as the most expensive shopping avenue in the world. It is said that all 50 leading brands worldwide can be found here, plus high-end brand stores owned and operated by celebrities themselves, earning it the reputation of "celebrity wardrobes". A must-visit when coming to Los Angeles.


Third Street - Santa Monica


Built around the Pacific Ocean, this is an outdoor pedestrian mall located near Santa Monica Beach. Besides complete fashion brand selection, it gathers over 80 restaurants, supermarkets, bookstores, coffee shops, and digital product stores - both high-end and everyday brands are available. Only a 5-minute walk from the beach, shopping, dining, and watching the sea make this perfect for spending an entire weekend day.


Melrose Avenue


This is Los Angeles's trendy street for branded fashion, and also a gathering place for social media influencers. The entire avenue extends about 10 kilometers, with all kinds of large, exaggerated graffiti symbolizing American freedom and wildness everywhere. For young people seeking fashion, Melrose Avenue is an absolute must-visit trend hub.


Desert Hills Outlets


Los Angeles's largest outlet mall, and also California's largest outlet. It boasts 180 designer specialty stores with significant discounts and a complete brand selection. Almost any major brands you can think of appear here, earning it the reputation as "a shopping paradise in the desert".


Conclusion


Los Angeles is a city that once visited makes people reluctant to leave. Its unique culture and charm attract countless visitors repeatedly. If you have more time, I suggest staying here longer to experience authentic California style and the more artistic "City of Angels"!
