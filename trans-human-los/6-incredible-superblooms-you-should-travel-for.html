Each year a rare natural phenomenon occurs at the Saguaro National Park and it is called a superbloom. They only occur about once every 10 to 15 years. This year the park experienced an exceptionally wet winter and now for the next few weeks their 40-foot saguaro cacti are in bloom. These cacti will have flowers on the tops and some will have creamy white blossoms that run along their entire length.

When to go: If you want to see the blooms on the cacti, your best bet will be the last week of May and the first week of June. If this short time window does not work for you, another option is from late February to about mid-April, which is also a very colorful time in the desert. Around this time, you can see the yellow brittlebush as well as a variety of different wildflowers scattered across the desert.
