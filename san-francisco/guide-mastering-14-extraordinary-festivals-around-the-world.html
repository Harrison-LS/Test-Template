Guide | Mastering 14 Extraordinary Festivals Around the World

I absolutely love participating in festivals around the world~


Participating in their festivals, being a local girl for a day~

It's also a unique travel experience!


1.January


Carnevale di Venezia


January 27 - February 13


Italy Venice | Venice Carnival


Venice Carnival is one of the oldest, largest, most romantic and splendid carnivals in the world today!

I associate it with those magnificent masks and costumes~


During the carnival, Venetians and tourists wear elaborate masks, dressing up as various legendary figures. Under the cover of masks, they indulge in celebration, as if attending a grand masked ball.


Poet Byron called it "Italy's supreme masquerade".


Delicious food, fine wine, balls, performances, parades, masks.


In this thousand-year-old ancient city of Venice, this is the most luxurious and mysterious carnival in all of Europe~


Location:

The Festa Veneziana, Venice, Italy


2.February


Rio Carnival


2/10 – 2/19


Brazil Rio de Janeiro | Rio Carnival


In fact, every February the entire South America is carnival hahaha but among them the most grandiose and also the most famous has to be the Rio Carnival!


The Rio Carnival with its number of samba dancers participating, gorgeous costumes, long duration, spectacular scenes can be called the best in the world!

!!

Adding Rio's unique enthusiasm to the mix, this festival turns the entire city into a "carnival king" that everyone participates in.


But watching the most beautiful girls and enjoying the lively parades requires buying tickets in advance. This is a grand celebration with people from all over the world coming to join the fun, and tickets are hard to get every year (and hotels are extremely difficult to book too!)!


So! Please prepare early!!!


Location:

Rio de Janeiro, Brazil (the entire city is partying)

3. March


Lathmar Holi


2/23 – 3/1


India | Beating Men Day


Beating Men Day generally starts about a week before Holi (the color festival), organized spontaneously by local women joining forces.


India has always been a country where men hold higher status than women, and this festival is the day for women to vent their frustrations!

So on this day, women will put on traditional saris, pick up sticks, and give passing men a good beating lol


Location:

2/23, 2018: Barsana Village (Barsana, Vrindavan)

2/24, 2018: Nandgaon Village (Nandgaon, Vrindavan)


Holi


3/1 - 3/2


India | Holi Festival


I went to this one lol (proud face)


Actually, Holi is India's New Year~

Locals prepare huge bags of colored powder and sprinkle it on people passing by! When I went there, mmp, a local guy chased our group and sprinkled us all along the way, so tired... Anyone who came by would smear my face with pink powder...


But this is indeed a "once in a lifetime" festival to experience!

Really interesting and crazy hahaha...


So, if you plan to go, book your itinerary and accommodation in advance! Because on that day, people from all over the world who love to join the fun will be there. I booked my accommodation on the last day before departure and nearly ended up sleeping on the street!


The festival is usually held at local temples in the morning~

After the temple celebration ends, everyone will rush to the streets and throw things around.


I recommend everyone prepare some colored powder beforehand; on that day, you'll see who gets covered! Come help me get revenge hahaha


tips:


Locations:

Mathura: Dwarkadheesh Temple

Vrindavan Village: Banke Bihari Temple


April 4


Songkran


4/13 – 4/15


Thailand | Water Festival


The Thai Water Festival is something everyone has probably heard of~ It's also Thailand's traditional New Year!

Everyone brings water guns, buckets to spray water on people around~ Why am I always so obsessed with this kind of battle-style celebration?


Anyway… I'll be going to this one this year! In April, when I go, I'll update you!


This festival is nationwide. Let me mention a few large water splash festival locations~


Tips:

1. Stay safe and watch your belongings! The celebration gets very crowded, leave important items at the hotel and don't carry them with you! Be careful of motorcycles while celebrating in the streets~

2. Watch out for cameras, bring a waterproof cover~

3. Wear sunglasses! Prevent water from getting into your eyes~


Locations:

Bangkok | Khao San Road & Silom Road

Chiang Mai | The Pae Gate

Phuket | Patong Beach


5.May


"Summer of Love"


May (specific time not announced)


USA San Francisco | "Summer of Love" Nude Parade


This festival, kids shouldn't be watching wildly haha!

Only we adults can watch~

Hahaha, just kidding~


San Francisco has always been a city where progressive and new ideas converge.

Every year, many LGBTQ+/hippie parade celebrations are held here!


This "Summer of Love" nude parade is one of them.


People in San Fran love nudity, it's become something quite normal now!


In March there's a nude cycling event,

and in May there's a fully nude parade.


Participants must be completely naked; if you only want to remove your upper body, you can only be a partial participant beside them...

The meaning of the event is to liberate bodies and help everyone have broader perceptions of aesthetics; every body type deserves appreciation.


Parade route:

from Jane Warner Plaza, North on Castro, West on Haight to Stanyan, return: East on Haight, South on Castro, back to Jane Warner Plaza.


6. June


Royal Ascot


6/19 – 6/23

London | British Royal Ascot Horse Racing


This is an ancient, noble, yet still fashionable royal horse racing event

Members of the British royal family also attend, so you might even run into a prince~ hahaha (regular people can buy tickets to enter)


Besides horse racing, the most important thing is the fashion gala on the third day called "Ladies Day"!

keyword: Royal Ascot


The Royal Ascot updates its dress code requirements for spectators annually.


In general, attendees are required to dress formally.

Gentlemen should wear black or grey morning coats with top hats; requirements for ladies are more detailed, with specific regulations on headwear, skirts, and even strap dimensions.


Additionally, ladies must wear large top hats; if the hat is deemed too small, they will be asked to purchase one on-site. Thus began this competition of fashionable women showing off their splendor... hehe...

July 7


San Fermín


7/6 - 7/14


Spain | Running of the Bulls Festival


The Running of the Bulls is one of Spain's most traditional and representative festivals. If you want to experience Spanish passion and enthusiasm, and feel Spain's traditional culture, then this festival is indeed a great choice!

San Fermín


It preaches a Spanish national sense of honor where "honor far outweighs life"!


The most famous bull-running festival is the San Fermin festival held annually on July 7th in Pamplona, Navarre.


Participants typically wear all white with red scarves around their necks. Many young people take this opportunity to join the crowd and run alongside bulls, showing off their courage~


For safety, it's recommended to arrive early and find a higher vantage point to watch the festivities~


Never stay in the crowd, as this festival frequently experiences dangerous incidents!


Location:

Pamplona, Madrid, Spain


August 8th


La Tomatina Festival


August 28 – August 29


Spain | "Tomato Battle" Festival


I must say, the Spanish people are a very lively and energetic nation hahaha. This is another boisterous celebration event~


What can I do? One of my biggest hobbies is "watching people fight".

Haha~


The Tomato Battle is a festival held annually on the last Wednesday of August.

Thousands of participants travel from around the world to join a harmless free-for-all, throwing over 120 tons of ripe, juicy tomatoes onto the streets.


Meanwhile, carnival parades take place on the town's main street, where you can throw tomatoes at the floaters hahaha


Location: Bunol, Spain

9.September


Burning Man


8/26 – 9/3


Nevada, USA | Burning Man Festival


I've wanted to go for this event for three or four years now but haven't made it ahhhhh I'm crazy! Either I'm not in the US or tickets are sold out. Whatever, I MUST GO THIS YEAR!!!


It's an annual event held in Nevada, USA in the Black Rock Desert～ The nine-day festival starts on the Sunday before and ends on Labor Day (the first Monday of September).


The event venue is a truly desolate desert, hahaha

Organizers will set up various magical wooden portraits throughout the site, of course plus 9 days of electronic music parties!


Hippies and young people from all over America and around the world (after all, they're all cool people) will dress up to attend! Most will rent RVs to enter the venue, since they'll be staying there for 9 consecutive days!


The name "Burning Man" comes from the ceremony of burning giant human-shaped wooden portraits on Saturday night, which is also the high point of the entire 9-day event!


Location: Black Rock Desert, Nevada, USA

Tickets & Event Details portal: ***


October 10


Oktoberfest


September 22 – October 7


Munich, Germany Beer Festival


This is a festival all about drinking~

Suitable for babies who love alcohol, hahaha!


During the beer festival there will also be parades. Parade participants will wear traditional costumes, there will be folk music, flower carriages, and so on~


Besides that, the most important thing is to eat, drink, and party! It lets you fully experience this nation's passion, boldness, and vitality.


Various appetizers for beer, beer tents, large barrels of beer transported by horse-drawn carriages, horse racing, shooting, street performance, various folk music...

(Of course there are also various local girls with curves, holding beers for you to drink together hahaha)


This festival fully preserves Bavarian folk style and customs, also called the world's largest folk carnival!


Location: station Theresienwiese or Schwanthalerhöhe, Munich, Germany

Ticket purchase and event details portal:***


11. November


Día de Muertos


October 31 – November 2


Mexico | Day of the Dead


Ever since I watched the movie "Coco", I've promised myself to go back to Mexico again, to participate in the Day of the Dead!


Day of the Dead is a traditional festival for the Mexican people~

In this festival period, Mexicans joyfully celebrate the completion of life cycles, welcoming once a year the reunion between living and deceased.


Singing and dancing all night long, intending to celebrate the festival together with departed relatives.


This festival can be said to be celebrated throughout Mexico~

Men, women, old and young dress up in skull costumes, paint skull faces on themselves, go out to celebrate happily, everyone is so happy hahaha~


Let me introduce a few major locations!


· Mexico City Mexico city

· Oaxaca state Oaxaca (located in southern Mexico)

· Janitzio & Patzcuaro, Michoacan (places that appeared in Coco)

· Miquihuila Mexico

· Merida, Yucatan

12. December


Christmas


12/25


Finland Santa Claus Village | Christmas


Christmas is a festival we celebrate every year!

But! If you have a chance, you must visit Santa Claus's homeland to experience Christmas there!!!


Santa Claus Village is a Finnish town near the Arctic Circle. Here you can find snow, reindeer sleds, aurora borealis, and of course the cheerful Finnish version of Santa Claus hahaha


There is a Christmas market, the legendary Santa Claus Post Office headquarters, and elves selling stamps, postcards, stationery, and various Christmas-themed souvenirs!


At night when lights are turned on, the small village becomes incredibly dreamlike, like a fairy tale~


Location: 96930 Rovaniemi, Finland
