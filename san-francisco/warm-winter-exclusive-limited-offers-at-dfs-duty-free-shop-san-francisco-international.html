Warm Winter - Exclusive Limited Offers at DFS Duty-Free Shop, San Francisco International Airport

The Director estimates many friends have travel plans during the November-December holiday season. How can you not visit duty-free stores when traveling? During the Thanksgiving and Christmas double celebration rush, how can you not shop, shop, shop? The thoughtful Director has written out this San Francisco International Airport DFS Duty-Free Store limited promotion guide before everyone departs, hoping everyone will use the money saved on tax-free purchases to eat more delicious food, hehe!


San Francisco International Airport


San Francisco International Airport

San Francisco International Airport is located approximately 13 miles south of downtown San Francisco City, bordering Millbrae and San Bruno in San Mateo County. It is a major commercial airport in California, USA, offering direct flights to major cities across North America, Europe, Asia, and Australia.


DFS Duty-Free Store


First Gift: DFS x Longines Special Watch


I believe many experienced watch enthusiasts know that Swiss watchmaking brand Longines partnered with DFS Group, the global leader in luxury travel retail. To celebrate their 50-year cooperation anniversary, they launched the Master Collection DFS Limited Edition Watch earlier this year.


DFS x Longines Special Watch


Available in two sizes, 40mm and 29mm, this timepiece features a sunray silver dial with black Roman numeral hour markers. It includes interchangeable stainless steel bracelet and brown alligator leather strap options, along with a folding safety clasp and transparent case back design.


DFS x Longines Special Watch


The DFS special edition watch is crafted exclusively for DFS, symbolizing the unwavering commitment to premium quality from both DFS and Longines. It represents a perfect modern embodiment of the traditions, elegance, and performance that the brand holds dear. As DFS continuously seeks new exclusive products for travelers, this Longines Master Collection DFS Special Edition watch stands as an ideal example.


This limited edition timepiece is now available at DFS Duty-Free in San Francisco International Airport until the end of 2019. Travelers who appreciate this distinctive watch can purchase it directly within the airport terminal. To enhance its unique luxury duty-free shopping experience for passengers, DFS Duty-Free at SFO offers additional incentives when purchasing a new timepiece:


🌟 T Membership Exclusive Perks: Purchase a Longines Master Collection - DFS Special Edition watch and receive a complimentary leather watch pouch as your exclusive gift.


🌟 In-store Gift: Purchase the DFS x Longines special edition watch and receive a brand exquisite fountain pen and limited leather case.
