Stay | San Francisco Hotel Booking Confidential Files

In travel, hotels are an important component that largely affects the overall comfort and experience of the trip.


Hardware


Let's see, when booking a hotel, what factors need to be comprehensively considered?

First and foremost is the safety of the area where the hotel is located; secondly, the hotel's geographical location, distance to downtown attractions and convenience of transportation; then whether the infrastructure around the hotel is complete, such as are there large supermarkets nearby for purchasing food and fruit? Are there plenty of restaurant choices nearby? And your specific arrival time in San Francisco are all factors that need to be considered~

Next, let's take a look at the area range of San Francisco:


1. San Francisco Airport


There are some hotel options near the airport. If your flight arriving at San Francisco International Airport on the first day is relatively late, since clearing customs and immigration requires about 1.5-2 hours, it is suggested to choose staying in an airport hotel.


2. San Francisco Downtown


San Francisco downtown concentrates a large number of hotels of different sizes and various grades, including many high-end hotels.

Advantages: Very close to city center attractions, convenient transportation, for example you can walk to Union Square for shopping etc.;

Disadvantages: Poor safety in the city center, expensive parking fees, some hotels don't even have their own parking lots, hotels are relatively old, rooms are relatively small.


3. San Francisco Bay Area


The San Francisco Bay Area is quite large, also need to pay attention to screening the safety of each city! For example, cities like Hayward, Union City, Newark, Fremont have good security and many hotel options.

Advantages: Most free parking, hotels are relatively new, convenient surrounding infrastructure, more large supermarkets and restaurants;

Disadvantages: About 20-30 minutes drive from city center

If your flight arriving at San Francisco airport is late, then staying overnight at an airport hotel is a very good choice.,
