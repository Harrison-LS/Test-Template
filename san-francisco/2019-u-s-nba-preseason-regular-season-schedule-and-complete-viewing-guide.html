2019 U.S. NBA Preseason/Regular Season Schedule and Complete Viewing Guide

What are the major questions you must know before reserving regular season tickets?


1. When does the NBA season start each year? How long does it last?


The regular season starts at the end of October or early November each year, ending around April 20th of the following year. With 30 teams playing a total of 1189 games, there are multiple games every day. The playoffs begin in late April and end by mid-June. As an elimination tournament, the 16 teams that enter this stage will eventually compete to determine 2 finalists for the championship game. Pre-season is not an official competition; each team conducts a one-month warm-up period before the regular season, typically without home games for each team. All-Star Weekend is also not an official competition—it's a showcase featuring NBA stars, including the Rookie Challenge, Skills Competition, and the main All-Star Game, usually held during some weekend in February.

Therefore, you can watch NBA games in America from October to June of the following year.


2. How far in advance can you reserve tickets?


Regular season tickets can be purchased starting before the season begins, and you have until the day before each game to buy them.

Playoff tickets go on sale after the regular season ends. Finals tickets are sold starting before the finals because it's not known in advance which team will make it to the finals or at which arena the game will be played. All-Star Weekend package tickets start being sold six months in advance.


3. How are NBA ticket prices?


Can we have fixed year-round prices? No. Although regular season dates are confirmed early, the price for each individual game is different. The playoffs and finals don't even confirm teams and venues in advance, so there won't be fixed prices.

Ticket prices range how much? Regular season ticket prices generally fall between 50-500 USD, with some popular team games potentially starting at 150 USD or more.

Playoff tickets are higher priced; hundreds to thousands of USD are normal, and finals tickets can even reach over 10,000 USD.

Which teams are most popular? Los Angeles Lakers, Los Angeles Clippers, Golden State Warriors (San Francisco), Boston Celtics, Cleveland Cavaliers, Houston Rockets, San Antonio Spurs, Dallas Mavericks, New York Knicks, Miami Heat, Toronto Raptors, Chicago Bulls


4. What areas correspond to ticket prices?


The audience seating in the arena is generally oval-shaped around the court, divided into 3 rings: the inner ring (commonly called "inner field" or "home court", areas starting with number 1), the middle ring (areas starting with number 2), and the outer ring (commonly called "on the mountain" or upper deck, areas starting with number 3).

The inner ring is most expensive, the outer ring is cheapest; the sides of the arena are most expensive, the ends (behind the baskets) are cheapest. But tickets for the ends of the inner ring are still more expensive than side seats in the outer ring.

Price differences can be expressed as:

Inner ring side seats > Inner ring corner seats > Inner ring behind basket seats > Middle ring side seats > Middle ring corner seats > Middle ring behind basket seats > Outer ring side seats > Outer ring corner seats > Outer ring behind basket seats

6. Will the price appear on the ball ticket? Is another ticket exchange needed at the venue?


Ball tickets are generally electronic, and typically there's no price displayed on them, but the purchaser's name will be shown, which doesn't affect usage. If it's a second-hand ticket, it might display the previous owner's name, but this also doesn't impact usability. When using, you need to print out the electronic ticket and scan the code for entry; no additional ticket exchange is required.


7. If a match is scheduled in your itinerary, how much time should be reserved for the game?


Official match duration is 12 minutes × 4 quarters = 48 minutes, with overtime periods at 5 minutes each. There's a 130-second rest between the first and second quarters, as well as between the third and fourth quarters. A 15-minute break occurs between the second and third quarters.

There is a 100-second break between the 4th quarter and overtime, as well as between any overtimes. Plus time for fouls, free throws, timeouts, substitutions, etc., a game takes about 2+ hours. Adding entry and exit time, it's recommended to allow at least 3.5-4 hours when planning your trip.


8. Other things to note when watching games?


Suites can be reserved? Besides regular seating, there are also private suites available. Suites are typically located between the middle and outer sections of the stadium. The viewing angle is not as good as the middle or inner rings, but they excel with independent space, comfortable seats, TVs for watching live games, plus drinks and food service. Suites come in different sizes, generally accommodating 6-30 people each. Per-person spending runs around $200-$500. Playoff and championship game prices are subject to inquiry.

Suites cannot be booked directly online; you need to contact the arena's sales department, so it's recommended to place your order at least one month in advance.

Is a ticket needed for regular entry to the stadium? When watching games, besides the ballgame ticket cost, are additional tickets required? The arena is generally not open to tourists on normal days unless under special circumstances, in which case authorization can be requested from relevant organizations. During game time, you only need to purchase the ballgame ticket with no other mandatory fees. Food and beverages inside the stadium are paid separately.

What should you know about watching games? How early should you arrive? Can you bring bags and food? Entry is allowed 90 minutes before tip-off. It's recommended to arrive early, as you can see star players practicing on-site; At this time, stadium management won't be so strict. If you're lucky, you might sneak into inner court seats and take commemorative photos with stars in the background. (Low probability)

You can bring bags into the arena, but they'll all go through security screening. Backpacks and professional photography equipment may be required to be stored. Food cannot be brought in (if you have special dietary habits or requirements, you need to provide proof or medical advice). There are many places selling food inside the stadium, mainly fast food and snacks, prices slightly higher.

Before the game, everyone stands up for the American national anthem to be played throughout the venue. Although we're not Americans, you should follow local customs and respect them, so you probably should also stand.

Dress code recommendations focus on casual styles that complement the relaxed entertainment atmosphere of the event.

When watching live, clearly identify home versus away teams! If supporting the home team, join in enthusiastically with most fans; they often wear their favorite player's jersey. For away team supporters, connect with like-minded fans. Keep excitement measured when your team scores, as being on someone else's turf means safety comes first.

When watching the game, maintain respectful behavior and watch civilizationally. Avoid littering during exciting moments, which could risk security removal from the venue. Always clean up personal trash when leaving.

4. What's the difference between watching live vs. on TV?


At the venue, you can not only personally feel the team's performance and have your mood fluctuate with their score, but also experience a different atmosphere from watching on TV. Americans are warm and open-minded; even strangers around you have a good chance of coming over to high-five or even hug you when their supported team scores.

The home team's mascots often come out to interact with fans, very affectionate; the opening performance and cheerleaders are also great to watch (not just looking at heads, bodies should be watched too); during halftime there will also be segments where the big screen interacts with the audience. Sometimes they play popular music, randomly selected audience members have to improvise a dance; sometimes it's "Kiss Time" or "Hug Time," where the screen randomly captures audiences who would ideally hug or kiss each other. Even if they're not really couples, most open-minded Americans will comply enthusiastically. This sense of being fully "immersed" in the game is definitely way more exciting than quietly watching a broadcast on TV at home. Even die-hard casual fans can easily blend into the experience.


5. What's the difference between NBA rules and international basketball standards?


Compared to other basketball competitions, the NBA places more emphasis on performance and commercial aspects, so its rules also differ somewhat from international conventions; here are some of them:

National Basketball Association (National Basketball Association, abbreviated as NBA)

International Basketball Federation (FIBA)

Court:

NBA courts are shorter and wider than FIBA courts;

The NBA restricted area is rectangular, while FIBA's is trapezoidal;

The three-point line in the NBA is 6.70 meters from the basket rim, while FIBA's is 6.25 meters.

Rules:

Each NBA game allows for 7 timeouts; FIBA permits 4 timeouts.

NBA timeout durations last 1 minute and 40 seconds each; FIBA timeouts are 1 minute long.

The personal foul limit in the NBA is 6 fouls before disqualification; FIBA's limit is 5 fouls.

There are typically 3 referees for a game in the NBA; FIBA generally uses 2 referees.

Many other differences exist and won't all be listed here. Remember one key point: many NBA regulations serve its commercial interests (advertisers).


6. How are the Eastern and Western conferences divided?

Is it possible that both teams in the finals come from the same conference (East or West)?


The NBA is divided into Eastern and Western Conferences, each with 15 teams. The Western Conference has three divisions: Northwest, Southwest, and Pacific. The Eastern Conference has three divisions: Southeast, Central, and Atlantic.

Western Conference Divisions:

Northwest: Jazz, Nuggets, Trail Blazers, Timberwolves, Thunder;

Southwest: Mavericks, Spurs, Rockets, Pelicans, Grizzlies;

Pacific: Suns, Lakers, Warriors, Clippers, Kings.

Eastern Conference Divisions:

Southeast: Heat, Wizards, Magic, Hornets, Hawks;

Central: Pistons, Cavaliers, Bulls, Pacers, Bucks;

Atlantic: Raptors, Nets, 76ers, Knicks, Celtics.

The finals is played between conference champions from the East and West, so it cannot be teams from the same conference.


What's the difference between regular season and playoffs?


The regular season involves every team participating. Teams in the same conference and division play each other twice home and away (4 games total). Teams from different conferences play once home and away (2 games each). Teams from the same conference but different divisions play 3-4 games, varying by opponent. This ensures each team plays a total of 82 regular season games.

After the regular season concludes, the top eight teams from each conference advance to compete in the playoffs, with the first-place team from each division automatically securing a spot in the top three playoff positions.

The playoffs employ a best-of-seven series format using a 2-2-1-1-1 home court allocation system. The team possessing four home games will host games one, two, five (if necessary), and seven (if needed). Games three, four, and six (if required) are played away. Home-court advantage between competing teams is not evenly distributed.

The playoffs consist of four rounds culminating in the Finals: the second round is called the Division Semifinals, the third round is the Division Finals. The victorious team becomes a conference champion. The Eastern and Western Conference champions advance to the Finals. The first team to win seven games claims the championship title for that season, with the player demonstrating the most outstanding performance among the championship team earning the Finals MVP honor.

In summary, all teams participate in the regular season, but only the top eight performers from each conference can qualify for the playoffs. One team from each conference will compete in the Finals.
