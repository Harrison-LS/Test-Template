San Francisco Bay Area | Fillmore Shopping Guide

Fillmore District is one of San Francisco's most attractive and entertainment-rich areas, where you can not only experience local culture and enjoy delicious food but also choose from abundant entertainment activities. If you want to buy travel souvenirs, Fillmore has many shops worth visiting! Today we have carefully selected four great shopping spots in Fillmore - let's take a look!


French Pastry Shop B. Patisserie


Address: 2821 California St.


B. Patisserie is a very special French-style pastry shop. After shopping around Fillmore, come here for a cup of coffee and dessert to eliminate the fatigue of the day! The shop's homemade gingerbread, ham and cheese croissants are signature items. Each season they launch limited-time coffee cakes that are delicious beyond praise. On weekends, you can also try popular macarons from French pastry, or apple pie handcrafted taking ten hours to make.


African Arts Museum


Address: 1305 Fillmore St.


This art gallery houses many extraordinary African treasures, founded by Solange Mallett from Côte d'Ivoire in Africa. The exhibits feature a series of textiles, beads, decorative arts, jewelry, and other artworks created by artists from the African continent. Visitors can also admire African tribal masks, sculptures, necklaces, and handmade artistic pieces such as traditional African clothing, distinctive handbags, and beaded items.


Browser Bookstore


Address: 2195 Fillmore St.


"Browser" means search or browse. As its name suggests, the Browser Bookstore is ideal for visitors who want to explore bookstores and find great books. Since 1976, it has attracted tourists from both local areas and around the world. The bookstore offers various subjects of literature, but its real treasure lies in its philosophy and religious collections. Browser has many Buddhist-related books as well as numerous volumes on Eastern and Western religions. If you are interested in this area, the Browser is a destination worth exploring.


Roam Artisan Burgers


Roam Artisan Burgers


Address: 1923 Fillmore St.


After a day of shopping, the best thing is to find a local delicacy to feast on. In Fillmore, Roam Artisan Burgers is an excellent choice. Located on Fillmore Street, it's considered to have the best burgers in Fillmore. This classic-style burger joint offers various flavors including beef, turkey, bison, veggie, or lamb, with all kinds of toppings available. Additionally, their handmade sodas and milkshakes are perfect accompaniments to burgers - truly a taste worth visiting for.
