Where to Stay in San Francisco? A Guide to Choosing the Right Neighborhood and Hotel

Accommodation in San Francisco: Things to Know in Advance


The chaos in San Francisco constantly reported on the news often makes travelers worry about San Francisco's "safety". Overall, downtown tourist attractions are concentrated with a relatively small activity range for visitors. During daytime there is no need to worry too much about personal safety, but still worth noting:


1. To avoid smash-and-grab theft from cars, it is suggested not to leave anything inside the vehicle; downtown it is best to take public transportation or ride-hail (Uber); "Overnight roadside parking" suffering broken window theft is very common in San Francisco, so suggest parking at hotel lots;

2. To reduce the possibility of robbery, it is suggested not to carry expensive bags, not to play on your phone by the streetside, not to walk alone at night; before dusk (8pm) you must definitely return to the hotel;

3. It is recommended to avoid choosing areas where homeless people congregate such as Tenderloin and Market Street for accommodation;

4. Be wary of scammers who ask you to take their photos, then demand dollars from you; even at Union Square you might encounter them, stay alert.


Accommodation Area Selection


The darker the color, the more frequent "crime" activity occurs.

Fisherman's Wharf is typically the first choice for self-driving travelers, with affordable parking and relatively better safety. It provides convenient access to attractions like the Palace of Fine Arts, 9-Arched Street, and Alcatraz Island.


Additionally, the area near Union Square is the top choice for many travelers using public transportation. Located in the heart of the city with numerous attractions, dining options, and shopping, Powell Street subway station and cable cars are all available. However, be sure to stay away from the Tenderloin district nearby.


Fisherman's Wharf area


For independent travelers, the Fisherman's Wharf area should be prioritized due to convenient travel. Although there are homeless individuals in this area, their numbers are relatively small. Car break-ins do occur but with lower frequency. The only drawback is its proximity to tourist areas and nightlife districts, which can be noisy. Overall accommodation prices tend to be higher with fewer choices available.


Fisherman's Wharf Marriott Hotel


A classic brand chain hotel with quality assurance. This is one of the few hotels where travelers can choose without research and unlikely make a wrong decision.

Near the hotel are photo spots like the Golden Gate Bridge, Pier 39, etc.


English name: San Francisco Marriott Fisherman's Wharf

Off-season per person: 1755 yuan/day

Self-driving related: Parking fee $73.08/day


Yiyi Bay Fisherman's Wharf Hotel


Suitable for family travel, besides an excellent location, it also ensures basic accommodation conditions, with extremely high value.


English name: Riu Plaza Fisherman's Wharf

Off-season per person: 900+ yuan/day

Self-driving related: Parking fee $60/day


Japanese Style San Francisco Hotel


Within a 5-minute walk to Fisherman's Wharf, a 3-minute walk to Lombard Street, excellent location.

The hotel features a nautical theme, has very high online ratings, is clean and interesting, and provides comfortable stays for both children and adults.


English Name: Hotel Zephyr San Francisco

Low Season Per Person: 1400+ yuan/day

Self-driving Related: Parking fee $74/day


Cow Hollow Inn and Suites


A chain hotel with excellent value near Fisherman's Wharf. Just the free parking alone is enough to attract many self-driving travelers.


English Name: Cow Hollow Inn and Suites

Low Season Per Person: 1100+ yuan/day

Similar Recommendations: Chelsea Inn, which also offers free parking


Union Square Area


Union Square area in the downtown is also a popular choice for most travelers. There are many hotels to choose from here, with restaurants and transportation convenience being self-explanatory. Even hotels at the same price range tend to be slightly cheaper than those in the Fisherman's Wharf area.

However, in contrast, safety is not as high; there will be more homeless people. However, usually if you avoid the nearby Tenderloin district and don't go out shopping at night, it will be much better.


Tips:

Additionally, considering that self-driving parking fees are very expensive, there are three well-reviewed parking spots nearby: Ellis-O'Farrell Garage ($2～6/hour, daily cap of $42), Mason O'Farrell Garage (first hour $8, daily cap of $36), and Union Square Garage (~$6/hour, daily cap of $36).


San Francisco Union Square Chancellor Hotel


A historic family-run hotel dating back to the 1915 Panama-Pacific Exposition. The hotel is located directly opposite Union Square, surrounded by shopping malls and restaurants, and close to the cable car station. Macy's Department Store is just a few steps away, making it extremely convenient for shopping, dining, and getting around.


English Name: Chancellor Hotel on Union Square

Cheap season reference price: $140+/night

Self-driving related: Parking fee of $35/day, which is very cheap in San Francisco


San Francisco Union Square Loews Hotel


A hotel opened in 2021 full of design flair. The creative colorful spaces inject fresh vitality for travelers.

The restaurant and bar are open 24 hours a day; room facilities are controlled by intelligent touch screens, making it quite comfortable to stay in.


English name: citizenM San Francisco Union Square

Off-season reference: 1100+ RMB/night

Self-driving related: The hotel has no parking lot; a parking lot one block away costs $44/day


Westin St. Francis Hotel San Francisco Union Square


Opened in 1904, this hotel is located directly in front of the Union Square cable car station. Renovated in 2014, it features not only historic rose-colored grandfather clocks with classical charm, but also modern amenities like Westin's signature Heavenly Beds to meet travelers' smart requirements. Notably, the wine tasting room is operated by Chateau Montelena winery—a major highlight.


English name: The Westin St. Francis San Francisco on Union Square

Off-season reference: 1400+ RMB/night

Self-driving related: $75/day


Hyatt Regency San Francisco SOMA


Located on Market Street in San Francisco's SOMA Arts District (those who mind the location should proceed with caution), it is near the Contemporary Art Museum, Yerba Buena Center for the Arts, and the San Francisco Museum of Modern Art (SFMOMA), among many excellent restaurants and coffee shops.


English Name: Hyatt Regency San Francisco Downtown SOMA

Low season reference: RMB 1800+ yuan/night

Self-driving related: Parking fee $85.5/day


San Francisco International Airport


San Francisco's public transportation is well-developed. Taking a taxi (Uber and Lyft) to downtown usually takes less than half an hour, with one-way fare around $35, which is cheaper than the hotel's nightly parking fee. If taking BART from near the airport, one-way is about $9. If self-driving is more suitable - airport hotels are almost at half the price of comparable downtown hotels, plus offering free parking and complimentary airport shuttle service.


SFO Dylan Hotel


There are supermarkets nearby along with multiple restaurants in an excellent location. Additionally, the BART station is just a 3-minute walk away, making travel very convenient.


English Name: The Dylan Hotel at SFO

Off-season reference: RMB 720+ / night

Key features: Free parking; provides free airport shuttle service


Bay Landing Hotel


Only a 6-minute drive from the airport. With restaurants nearby making dining convenient, this property offers great value for money and service in this area.


English Name: Bay Landing Hotel

Off-season reference: RMB 720+ / night

Key features: Free parking; provides free airport shuttle service
