Home Sweet Home

On Monday, July 13, we wrapped up our trip. The main focus of this day was our long drive back home. We spent about six hours on the road before we finally returned to Omaha, Nebraska, and then arrived back at home sweet home.

There’s a bit of everything when it comes to the conclusion of a trip. While we’re all sad to see it end, we had a great time, and will always remember the time we spent together. While the boys will be excited to start summer activities, it will be nice for everyone to be able to sleep in their own beds again. There is still a lot of summer to enjoy in Minnesota!

With breakfast done and the last bit of packing complete, we got on the road again. Pizza Rach places are rare out west so we decided that stopping at one would be a good last lunch pick in Iowa. The boys agreed easily. 😁

During our drive home we spent over 4,550 miles in the van with lots of great bonding time. We passed through seven states (UT, CO, AZ, NM, NE, IA, MN) and saw seven national parks, a national monument, and even a state park. We arrived home safe on Monday at about 4:30. Over the course of the trip, we managed a single flat tire, temps ranging from 33 to 106 degrees, and above all, created countless great memories. We’re grateful that God kept us safe.

The trip this year had its challenges, but there was so much to be grateful to God for.  Each family trip has to have some fun first time experiences, lessons learned, and family memories to take with us. Flat tire, crazy heat, and some not so happy moments (from kids and parents).

One of the most surprising questions came from Josiah. He asked, "Is there love in Oklahoma?" This is actually a line from Westward Whoa!, the school play that the older boys acted in this year. It is a mystery why this kept coming up during the trip. 🤪

Lessons From This Trip:

1. Peter learned a tire patching technique that kept us mobile, at least in the short run.

2. Gen Alpha children can be entertained by even very old cartoons.

3. IHOP has a kids eat free special every day.

4. When temperatures exceed 100 a "dry heat" is only marginally better.

5. Utah’s Mighty 5, Mesa Verde National Park, Great Sand Dunes National Park, Cedar Breaks National Monument, and Goblin Valley State Park should also be included.

92661540-7f9e-11f1-867c-65474638af11.jpeg

Now, the biggest question is where to go next summer. The boys have already suggested an impressive list of options. Elam wants to go to China, Ezra is suggesting to Chicago for the MLB All-Star Game, Micah wants to go to Chuck E. Cheese in Minnesota, and Josiah is proposing Treasure Island in Minnesota. It seems like the planning committee is very creative!

Home is a blessing, but summer brings exciting opportunities, possibilities and adventures. With God's grace, we hope to explore more of his creation. We will treasure family time and the new memories we will create together. We will appreciate all life blessings until the next travel adventure.","I hope we will have the opportunity to travel.
