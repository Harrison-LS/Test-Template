It's already 2018, are you still planning to line up in long queues for 'Black Friday'?

Starting from Thanksgiving, the United States enters its annual shopping discount season. Items that people usually couldn't bear to buy can now be purchased at rock-bottom prices, while major shopping malls are packed with crowds and very lively. Cities of different styles also have discounts everywhere, and many people queue up for purchases regardless of rain or snow. Whether you prefer mainstream brands or niche brands, this is the best time to stock up.


Preface


Black Friday 2018 date: November 23, 2018

The following are several must-visit destinations in San Francisco for "Black Friday" recommended for everyone:


Union Square


When talking about the core shopping and strolling destination in San Francisco, the first thing that comes to mind is Union Square!

This fashionable shopping district serves as a premier outdoor retail haven in the Bay Area. Shoppers can find luxury brands from around the globe here - high-end names like Hermes, LV, Gucci, Chanel, along with accessible options such as Longchamp and Coach. Budget-friendly retailers including Uniqlo, Zara, and Forever 21 are also available for those seeking more affordable choices.

This fashionable shopping district is truly an outdoor shopping paradise in the Bay Area, featuring designer brands from around the world. From high-end Hermès, Louis Vuitton (LV), Gucci, Chanel to Longchamp, Coach - all are available here. Of course, there are also affordable shopping brands.

Such as Uniqlo, Zara, Forever 21 and more.

2018 Shopping Season Hours of Operation: Each store in the plaza has different operating hours, please check their official websites for details.


Union Square

Union Square is located in the bustling downtown area. At the center stands a tall monument erected to commemorate the U.S. Navy's victory over the Spanish fleet in Manila Bay. The square is surrounded by four streets, lined with unique shops and luxury department stores. Besides the two major department stores I. Magnin and Macy's, there are hundreds of high-end designer boutiques and fashion retailers - truly a shopper's paradise. Additionally, San Francisco's theater district lies just southwest nearby. Even if you don't plan to make purchases, simply strolling around will delight you with constant surprises.


Westfield San Francisco Centre


Westfield San Francisco Centre is located on Market Street in the heart of downtown San Francisco. It's not only a must-visit shopping destination for many tourists, but also a popular shopping center for locals.

Westfield combines major department stores like Nordstrom and Bloomingdale's, featuring top American popular and luxury brands such as Bottega Veneta, Louis Vuitton, plus well-liked brands including Hugo Boss, Tumi, Kate Spade, Tory Burch, Superdry, and others.

At the end of each year many stores offer discounts on top of their regular sales. Places like Abercrombie & Fitch, Club Monaco, and Bebe are worth exploring thoroughly. During discount seasons there are reductions across home goods, clothing, jewelry, and more. I recommend checking each store's official website for the most current details.

2018 Holiday Shopping Hours: Closed on Thanksgiving Day November 22nd, Friday November 23rd open from 7am-10pm


Union Street Shops


San Francisco has several distinctive shopping districts. Beyond the well-known Union Square area, there are many unique outdoor shopping zones featuring boutique stores, specialty shops, vintage retailers, bookstores, and cafes.

Union Street is a street on the north side of San Francisco, right next to the Marina District which has many high-end restaurants. The street is home to many restaurants, coffee shops, jewelry stores, and boutique shops - it's a place where you can spend an entire afternoon shopping. Must-visit include Jo Malone flagship store, BCBG, Sephora, etc. There are also special Thanksgiving discounts! Also remember to visit Wrecking Ball Coffee Roasters, one of San Francisco's specialty coffee shops. The small shop is cute and the coffee is rich and exquisite!

2018 Shopping Season Hours: Each store in the plaza has different operating hours, please check their official websites.


Stanford Shopping Center


Stanford Shopping Center in Palo Alto is the most beautiful outdoor shopping center in the Bay Area. It's a well-planned outdoor mall with various flowers planted throughout - wisteria, cherry blossoms, tulips, morning glories, and more. Every corner features comfortable seating areas with umbrellas for guests to relax, plus charging stations available!

Here you'll find over 150 brand stores, including popular ones like Apple Store, Free People, Microsoft, Muji, Stuart Weitzman, Tesla, Tory Burch, and also combined with Nordstrom, Neiman Marcus, Macy'

s Bloomingdale and other large shopping stores, whether or not it's Black Friday shopping festival, will definitely make you leave satisfied.

2018 Shopping Season Hours: Monday through Friday 10am-9:00pm; Saturday 10am-7pm; Sunday 11am-6pm; 2018 Thanksgiving Black Friday hours not yet announced.


Stonestown Galleria


Stonestown Galleria is located on the southwest side of San Francisco, near Daly City and Sunset Chinese area, with more Chinese people. Here you'll find many brands and restaurants popular among Asians, such as 85°C Bakery Cafe, Marugame Udon, soon-to-open Shilin Taiwan snacks, Gram-Cafe & Pancakes from Japan, etc.

Additionally there are Zara, Uniqlo, and recently opened MINISO (Minsu), all very worth visiting. During Black Friday and Christmas periods there are many discount areas, plus connected Nordstrom and Target for combined shopping.

2018 Shopping Season Hours: November 22 Thanksgiving 6pm-12am, November 23 Friday 6am-10pm

This place has over 130 popular retail stores, such as Nordstrom, Macy'

San Francisco Premium Outlet


San Francisco Premium Outlets is located in Livermore on the east Bay Area of Northern California. It's the largest outlet center closest to downtown San Francisco, accessible by public transportation.

San Francisco Premium Outlets opened in 2013 and has welcomed more brands in recent years, making it increasingly popular among visitors.

There are nearly two hundred stores here with a semi-outdoor block design. The walkways between shops are open-air, making it very comfortable to browse around.

Must-visit stores include Bottega Veneta, Coach, Michael Kors, Kate Spade, Burberry, Furla, Salvatore Ferragamo, Gucci, Tory Burch, Tod's

s, Roger Vivier, Longchamp, Prada, Superdry.

Finally, don't forget to enjoy a scoop of Amorino ice cream from Europe!

2018 Shopping Season Hours: Monday through Saturday 10am-9:00pm; Sunday 10am-7:00pm; 2018 Thanksgiving Black Friday hours have not yet been announced.
