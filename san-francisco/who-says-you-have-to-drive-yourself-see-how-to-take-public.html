Who says you have to drive yourself? See how to take public transportation to Yosemite!

Today I'm sharing a guide plan for traveling from "San Francisco" to the US National Park "Yosemite", introducing how to get there using public transportation.


Why choose public transportation?


The benefits of taking public transportation are saving effort, safety, and using WiFi. Another particularly big benefit is not needing to install snow chains, because the month/season differs, "Yosemite" has road closures and forced snow chain installation situations. What are snow chains? Good question, please see below.


Snow Chains


How to take transportation?


1. San Francisco --- Emeryville Station


First, arrive at "Emeryville Station" (Emeryville station), the specific route depends on where you're staying in "San Francisco", generally it takes about one hour by electric train and bus from within the city to reach the destination.


2. Emeryville Station --- Merced Station


After arriving at Emeryville Station, you need to purchase an Amtrak train ticket from 'Emeryville Station' (Emeryville station) to 'Merced Station' (Merced, CA).


Amtrak: ***

The link above is a website for purchasing Amtrak train tickets in North America (United States and Canada). If you're interested, you can check out relevant information about Amtrak. It's actually quite interesting; unlike our country, the United States never had an era of state-owned railways, and today's 'Amtrak' is also a collection of private railways.


Click to enter the page and select information. One-way or round-trip, departure location, destination, time, number of passengers, luggage, etc. Although this is a Chinese webpage, it can only recognize English information, and there are many repeated English place names, so please be careful not to fill in incorrect information.


After searching, different train schedules at various times will appear. The author chose the 7:45 AM departure train.

Personal insurance can be purchased here, or not—it is entirely voluntary at $9.


Of course, besides buying train tickets online, you can also purchase them at the counter; however, to avoid long lines and missing your departure time, be sure to allow ample time.

Amtrak trains feature dining cars, spacious seating, and most importantly usable WiFi—something that would frustrate even a casual smartphone user. From experience, the scenery on these trains is quite nice as well.


3. Merced Station --- YARTS Bus --- Yosemite


After arriving at "Merced Station" (Merced, CA), we finally reach this final step: transfer to the YARTS bus heading toward Yosemite.

This is a large air-conditioned bus equipped with restrooms, power outlets for iPhones, tablets and other devices, seat belts, plus luggage compartments for storing larger items.

YARTS Bus official website: ***


The blue line shown in the diagram below indicates the route; each point marked along this line represents a bus stop.


YARTS buses sell tickets on board, so you don't need to worry about not being able to purchase a ticket. Additionally, where can you board? See the diagram below—all these locations allow bus boarding.


Of course, there are also YARTS buses from other cities to Yosemite; you can refer to the official website for more information. After arriving at Yosemite, whether you plan to return on the same day or stay a few days before leaving requires advance planning. Yosemite entrance tickets are valid for seven days after purchase, so please decide accordingly based on your needs. This guide focuses on round-trip methods from San Francisco to Yosemite and does not provide an in-depth exploration of activities at Yosemite.


How to return?


Naturally, you can take a YARTS bus back to "Merced Station" (Merced, CA), then continue your journey by train. The process is exactly the same as when heading to Yosemite.
