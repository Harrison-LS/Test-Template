I. Should You Choose East Coast or West Coast for Your First Visit?


Most friends who want to visit America, after obtaining their US visa, typically have the following mental image of America in their minds:


American West: Drive through the sleepless city of Las Vegas, admire the majestic Grand Canyon, experience San Francisco filled with humanistic atmosphere, visit Los Angeles - the modern film hub - and relax in San Diego. Soak yourself in natural beauty composed of the magnificent Grand Canyon and Highway 1, plus the towering peaks and dramatic cliffs of Yosemite National Park.

West Coast is a wonderful destination to visit at any time of year. Generally, July and August are the peak tourist seasons on the West Coast, when all national parks are open. Choosing spring or autumn for your West Coast trip is also an excellent option; the summer heat has subsided, there are fewer tourists, and it's more cost-effective. The Eastern United States: The East Coast features more cultural and historical attractions, but it also boasts natural wonders like Niagara Falls and the Great Smoky Mountains. If you're interested in American history and politics, you definitely shouldn't miss the historic cities of the East Coast. Additionally, if you choose to visit the U.S. during winter, consider going to Florida; there it's warm as summer with brilliant sunshine. You'll find Orlando, the world's top theme park city, and Miami with its romantic, exotic charm. Immerse yourself in Disney World and Universal Studios—the happiest places on Earth—then head to the Everglades to get close to subtropical wildlife, before concluding your journey at West Key Largo where you can do exactly what pleases you. The East Coast offers intoxicating views of both natural landscapes and cultural sights throughout the entire trip. ## II. What Can You Do in Western America? ### 1) Natural Scenery [Highway 1] Highway 1 is a road that absolutely no one visiting the West Coast will miss. It features blue skies, azure seas, sunshine, and dramatic cliffs. Black rocks are artfully scattered among the crashing waves.

Drive south from San Francisco for 40 minutes to reach the famous Stanford University. Continue southwest for another hour and a half to enter Monterey, one of Highway 1's premier towns. Here you'll find a charming 17-mile coastline, the ultra-luxurious Cypress Point Golf Course, an aquarium perfect for family education, and uniquely characterful Cannery Row. Only about 20 minutes' drive away is another quiet and beautiful town, Carmel. You can combine visits to Carmel Mission, Carmel Plaza, and more.

Heading south from Monterey will take you past the scenic Big Sur Park, the Danish village of Solvang, before arriving in Santa Barbara. Santa Barbara is filled with Spanish charm; Stern's Pier, Coastal Park, and Santa Barbara Mission are all wonderfully picturesque destinations. There's also fresh seafood, Latin-style souvenirs, making this an essential stop on your road trip.

Highway 1 Self-Driving Tips:

1. This stretch of highway must be driven from north to south, starting from San Francisco and heading toward Los Angeles. When driving in this direction, the highway runs along the coastline side. Driving the opposite way means most roadside viewing platforms won't be accessible for stopping.

2. Never simply use automatic navigation from San Francisco to Los Angeles as your only route. Set up intermediate stops along the way. Because automatic navigation tends to select State Route 101 instead of California Highway 1, you'll miss the highlights. Additionally, many distinctive and memorable small towns and natural landscapes require manual location settings. It's best to navigate stop by stop.

Even if time is tight and you can't visit every attraction, it's best to navigate from place to place according to their locations, ensuring you're actually driving on California Route 1 so as not to miss some coastal scenery.

3. California sun is famously intense (especially at sunrise and sunset), safety first—you definitely need a good pair of sunglasses for this drive. Polarized lenses are preferred for driving, but wearing polarized lenses can make it hard to see the navigation screen, so the friend in the passenger seat must really focus on reading the navigation and watching the road.


[Yellowstone National Park]

What makes Yellowstone National Park most fascinating is the diversity of its landscape: canyons, waterfalls, high mountains (almost covered with snow year-round), boreal forests, lakes, rivers, grasslands, plus unique geology formed by volcanic activity. It's essentially a microcosm of American national parks—a distillation of their best features—waiting to offer new surprises everywhere.

Actually Yellowstone is open year-round, but from October through April the following year, many roads within the park are closed and many attractions shut down, making it very inconvenient; the park isn't fully open at that time (only partially open, causing you to miss some sights), so I recommend not going then. Generally from May through September the park is completely open, and that's the best time to visit Yellowstone.

Yellowstone has mainly five sightseeing areas concentrated with attractions:

1. The geothermal area in the southwest, featuring extensive hot springs, warm baths, steam vents, mud pots, and geyser holes;

2、The northwest section mainly features Norris Geyser Basin and Mammoth Hot Springs;

3、The central area is the canyon zone, where Yellowstone Canyon and waterfalls are located;

4、The northeast is the Roosevelt area, preserving old Western landscapes;

5、The southeast is the Yellowstone Lake area, primarily featuring West Thumb crater and Yellowstone Lake;

Traffic Tips:

1、Yellowstone National Park has five entrances: east, west, south, north, and northeast. Departing from Salt Lake City, if you want to arrive quickly, choose the west entrance by entering the park via West Yellowstone. The route is: Salt Lake City――Idaho Falls――West Yellowstone. From Salt Lake City to Idaho Falls is Highway 15, about a 4-hour drive with faster speeds but average scenery. From Idaho Falls to West Yellowstone is an ordinary road with excellent road conditions and beautiful scenery along the way.

2、Grand Teton National Park is not far from Yellowstone's south entrance. After descending Signal Mountain in Grand Teton National Park, continue north via Jr. Memorial Parkway and enter through Yellowstone's south gate.

There is a small waterfall at Moose Falls along the roadside, where you can actually spot moose nearby. Follow Lewis River past Lewis Lake and Grand Village to reach the park's 8-shaped core area.

3. Within Yellowstone National Park, roads form an "8" shape, spanning approximately 150 miles. Most scenic attractions are concentrated along this 8-shaped route. The park is vast with scenery everywhere - you'll need to stop frequently to admire and photograph the views. I recommend staying for 2-3 days.


2) Cityscapes

[Los Angeles]

Los Angeles is America's second super metropolis, rivaling New York in stature. It represents almost the entire American West Coast and is famous for having Hollywood. The energetic Universal Studios, Disney Land suitable for all ages, star-studded Beverly Hills, internationally renowned Walk of Fame, relaxed romantic Santa Monica Beach, and the electric Lakers home court - these are unique charms of Los Angeles, considered by many as the dream city.


[San Francisco] San Francisco is a major port on California's West Coast and one of America's most famous cities. It's renowned for its striking red Golden Gate Bridge, colorful Crooked Hill Street, and Fisherman's Wharf filled with delicious seafood. Home to prestigious universities like Stanford and Berkeley, positioned right next to Silicon Valley - truly an incubator of geniuses.


【Las Vegas】

Las Vegas is the brightest star in Nevada's desert, home to the world's most luxurious casinos. Along the main street, top-tier hotels line up—Venetian, MGM, Trump (yes, that same Trump), Bellagio, LV Paris, New York-New York, and many more. Each hotel here also operates a casino. Beyond real money gambling, these casinos offer entertainment too—shows, shopping, dining all available. Visiting Vegas means experiencing "living luxuriously."


III. What to do in the eastern US?


1) Cityscapes

【New York】The world's most famous super metropolis—New York. This is a place where all adjectives fall short of describing it accurately. In Director Jiang Wen's early classic film "Beijing People in New York," there's a memorable line: If you love someone, bring them to New York; if you hate someone, also bring them to New York. Here you'll find the Statue of Liberty, Empire State Building, Broadway, Times Square, Yankees, Wall Street, Brooklyn Bridge, Metropolitan Museum of Art, Central Park, Fifth Avenue, and many more.


【Washington】Washington is the U.S. capital and the only special district not affiliated with any state.

History enthusiasts will be captivated by the White House, Capitol Building, Lincoln Memorial, World War II Memorial, and various national museums here. Additionally, the Space Museum is a must-visit attraction for aviation and military history lovers.

【Boston】Located north of New York City, Boston is one of America's oldest and most historically significant cities. It is home to two world-renowned universities - Harvard University and MIT. The Freedom Trail tells the story of American historical evolution, while Quincy Market features globally famous Boston lobster. Whether you're a history buff, college student, or food lover, this city can satisfy you.


IV. What transportation method is recommended?


America is a country built on wheels, so traveling by self-driving in the US can be said to be a relaxed and enjoyable way. Before visiting America, one might think it could function like Taiwan, Hong Kong, or Southeast Asia - where public transportation could solve independent travel needs. In reality, this perception is completely reversed. In America, people use public transportation at very low rates compared to other regions. The public transportation system isn't as developed as in China, so choosing rental car self-driving is the most suitable option for independent travelers.


Advantages of driving in America: 1) Driving direction matches that of China; highways are wide and well-maintained with clear road signs

2) Most highways across the western United States have no toll fees.

There are some toll roads in Florida; if renting a car for Florida is recommended to purchase SunPass, a card specifically for Florida highways. When passing through toll stations just use the SunPass lane, saving you from queuing to pay and no need to worry about being fined.


Driving tips【License Notarization】Most US states recognize Chinese driving licenses. But because each state has different laws, usage varies. For well-known California, Arizona home of Grand Canyon, Nevada with Las Vegas, and several states around Yellowstone that you're planning to self-drive in, just bring your new version Chinese license directly usable; now the new version of Chinese license already has bilingual English-Chinese translation, even for old versions you can translate it yourself.

【Carry Documents】Prepare in advance all drivers' original licenses (license obtained within past year and still valid), notarized or translated copies of licenses (or International Driving Permit for foreigners), international credit card with sufficient limit under the primary driver's name, passport

【Traffic Rules】

1、STOP SIGN Stop sign: Many intersections in US don't have traffic lights; everyone relies on red STOP signs at intersections to stop and proceed. When multiple directions all display STOP signs, whoever arrives last must halt first; after stopping momentarily before restarting, if vehicles from different directions reach the intersection simultaneously, right-hand rule applies for priority.

2. Speed Limit: When seeing SPEED LIMIT signs, it's best to strictly follow the posted speed limit. However, based on experience, American traffic laws judge speeding based on actual road conditions. For example, even though a SPEED LIMIT sign shows 60 mph, most vehicles actually drive at 80 mph; in such cases, driving at 80 mph is also acceptable. So how do you determine if you're speeding? The best method is to "follow the flow" - maintain about the same speed as other drivers, and you'll have no problems. Additionally, America has almost no cameras, but police officers hide by roadsides. If you find yourself in front of all vehicles, or significantly faster than cars beside you, then a traffic stop might be imminent.

3. Red Light Right Turns: In America, right turns on red are permitted, but if you think it's the same as domestic rules where you can just "whoosh" and turn immediately, then you're very mistaken. In America, making a right turn at a red light requires one prerequisite - "MAKE COMPLETE STOP," meaning you must come to a complete stop before turning right. First stop your vehicle, confirm safety, then proceed with the right turn. Some intersections have NO RIGHT TURN ON RED signs; in those cases, right turns are prohibited during red lights. Remember that even if there's not a single person on the road, when encountering a red light sign you must still stop first before turning. The secret is "stop at the red light, count silently to three, then turn right."

[Parking] Parking I consider one of the most important aspects of renting a car. Pay special attention to signs - it's best not to miss any single sign, because errors are likely.

By the roadside, with signs pointing and markings on the ground. In popular areas of big cities, you can choose to park in parking lots, which are convenient, safe, and easy to operate.

1. Curbside Parking: If there is someone in the car, it doesn't count as parking; so when going to a small shop for drinks or snacks, you can leave one person in the vehicle. A red-painted curb along the road means no parking at any time. If the curb has no color and there are no markings on the ground and no parking signs, you may temporarily park briefly, but be sure to avoid garage entrances and small slopes along the road.

2. Park Meters: Parking meters are more cost-effective for shorter stops. Most accept credit cards with MasterCard or Visa logos. You first select how long you want to park, then pay with your credit card to start the timer.

3. Parking Garages: Suitable for longer parking durations or situations where curbside parking isn't available in popular big cities. These are paid facilities and come in two types: the first is direct manual payment by attendants; the second is self-service automated parking, which requires you to pay with your credit card at the machine before taking the car away. The operation involves first inserting the parking ticket into the machine, then inserting your credit card. After removing it, the screen displays the parking fee. Once the deduction succeeds, the parking ticket will pop up. You then take the ticket and drive out of the garage.
